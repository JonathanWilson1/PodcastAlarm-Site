<p>Conspiracy theories are fascinating whether you believe them or not. The best conspiracy podcasts dig into the evidence, separate fact from fiction, and ask the questions that mainstream coverage skips over. These 8 shows do exactly that — some sceptical, some open-minded, all worth a listen.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Tin+Foil+Hat+Sam+Tripoli">1. Tin Foil Hat with Sam Tripoli</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/tin-foil-hat.jpg" alt="Tin Foil Hat with Sam Tripoli podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Comedian Sam Tripoli brings wild energy and genuine curiosity to conspiracy theories, UFOs and fringe topics. The comedy angle makes even the most out-there subjects entertaining, and his guests range from fellow comedians to serious researchers.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Conspirituality+Podcast">2. Conspirituality</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/conspirituality.jpg" alt="Conspirituality podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Three journalists examine the overlap between conspiracy theories and wellness culture — anti-vax influencers, QAnon yoga teachers, crypto gurus selling enlightenment. It's a sharp, well-researched show that explores how misinformation spreads through communities you wouldn't expect.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Conspiracy+Show+Richard+Syrett">3. The Conspiracy Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/the-conspiracy-show.jpg" alt="The Conspiracy Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Richard Syrett interviews researchers, authors and insiders about the world's biggest conspiracies. He leans more toward the believer side, which makes for fascinating interviews with people you won't hear on mainstream shows.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Why+Files">4. The Why Files</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/the-why-files.jpg" alt="The Why Files podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>AJ dives into conspiracies, mysteries and the unexplained with a healthy dose of scepticism and actual research. Episodes are entertaining and well-produced, walking through each theory step by step before revealing what the evidence actually supports. Think Mythbusters for conspiracy theories.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Those+Conspiracy+Guys">5. Those Conspiracy Guys</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/those-conspiracy-guys.jpg" alt="Those Conspiracy Guys podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Gordon Rochford's marathon episodes (often 3-4 hours) go absurdly deep on each topic. From MK Ultra to the Illuminati, nothing is surface level. If you want to feel like you've done a degree in a conspiracy theory by the end of one episode, this is your show.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Higherside+Chats">6. The Higherside Chats</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/the-higherside-chats.jpg" alt="The Higherside Chats podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Greg Carlwood interviews fringe researchers and alternative thinkers on topics from secret space programmes to ancient civilisations. He's a skilled interviewer who lets his guests make their case without interruption. Open-minded without being gullible.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Coast+to+Coast+AM">7. Coast to Coast AM</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/coast-to-coast-am.jpg" alt="Coast to Coast AM podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The legendary late-night radio show hosted by George Noory covers UFOs, conspiracies, the paranormal and everything in between. It's been running since 1984 and the podcast feed gives you the best segments. A staple for anyone who stays up late wondering about the unknown.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Last+Podcast+on+the+Left">8. Last Podcast on the Left</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-conspiracy-podcasts/last-podcast-on-the-left.jpg" alt="Last Podcast on the Left podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ben Kissel, Marcus Parks and Henry Zebrowski cover serial killers, cults, UFOs and conspiracy theories with exhaustive research wrapped in dark comedy. Their conspiracy episodes are some of their best work — deeply researched and genuinely hilarious.</p>
