<p>From wilderness survival skills to long-distance hiking stories, these podcasts are for anyone who feels most alive outside. Whether you're prepping for a backcountry trip or just dreaming about one from your desk, these are worth your time.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=MeatEater+Podcast">1. MeatEater Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/meateater.jpg" alt="MeatEater Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Steven Rinella and crew cover hunting, fishing, conservation, and wild food in long, rambling conversations that feel like sitting around a campfire. The show is equal parts outdoor education and great storytelling.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Survivalist+Prepper">2. Survivalist Prepper</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/survivalist-prepper.jpg" alt="Survivalist Prepper podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dale covers preparedness without the doomsday hype. Episodes focus on practical skills like water purification, food storage, home security, and emergency planning. If you want to be better prepared without going full bunker, this is the show.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Wild+Ideas+Worth+Living">3. Wild Ideas Worth Living</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/wild-ideas.jpg" alt="Wild Ideas Worth Living podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Shelby Stanger interviews people who've turned their passion for the outdoors into a life — athletes, entrepreneurs, and adventurers who took the leap. The stories are inspiring without being preachy, and the REI backing means great production quality.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Dirtbag+Diaries">4. The Dirtbag Diaries</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/dirtbag-diaries.jpg" alt="The Dirtbag Diaries podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Beautifully produced stories from people who've chosen adventure over comfort. Each episode is a mini documentary about climbing, paddling, biking, or simply living close to the land. Patagonia sponsors it, which tells you the quality level.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Rewild+Yourself">5. Rewild Yourself</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/rewild-yourself.jpg" alt="Rewild Yourself podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Daniel Vitalis explores what it means to live closer to nature — foraging, hunting, water sourcing, and reconnecting with the wild skills our ancestors relied on. It's a thoughtful take on self-reliance that goes beyond typical survival content.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Joe+Rogan+Experience">6. The Joe Rogan Experience (Outdoor Episodes)</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/joe-rogan.jpg" alt="The Joe Rogan Experience podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Rogan's outdoor and hunting episodes with guests like Steven Rinella, Cameron Hanes, and Donnie Vincent are some of the best long-form outdoor content anywhere. The conversations range from bowhunting to conservation to wilderness philosophy, and they go deep.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Backpacker+Radio">7. Backpacker Radio</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/backpacker-radio.jpg" alt="Backpacker Radio podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>If you've ever thought about thru-hiking the Appalachian Trail, Pacific Crest Trail, or Continental Divide Trail, this is the podcast. Chatter and Twig interview hikers, review gear, and share trail news with infectious enthusiasm.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Anchored+with+April+Vokey">8. Anchored with April Vokey</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-survival-outdoor-podcasts/anchored.jpg" alt="Anchored with April Vokey podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>April Vokey is a fly fishing guide turned podcast host who covers conservation, outdoor skills, and off-grid living. Her interviews go deep — she's not afraid to spend two hours with a guest when the conversation is good.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
