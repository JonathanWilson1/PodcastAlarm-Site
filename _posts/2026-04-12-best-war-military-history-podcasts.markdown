<p>War and military history podcasts have become one of the richest corners of the medium. Long-form storytelling suits the subject perfectly — you can spend 4 hours with Dan Carlin on a single battle and still want more. These 8 shows cover conflicts from ancient Rome to the 21st century.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Hardcore+History+Dan+Carlin">1. Hardcore History</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/hardcore-history.jpg" alt="Hardcore History podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dan Carlin's multi-hour deep dives into history's most dramatic moments are in a class of their own. His series on World War I, the Mongol Empire and the fall of the Roman Republic are some of the best audio content ever made. Episodes come out rarely but they're worth the wait.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Revolutions+podcast+Mike+Duncan">2. Revolutions</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/revolutions.jpg" alt="Revolutions podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Mike Duncan traces the great revolutions of the modern world — English, American, French, Haitian, Russian and more. Each series runs for dozens of episodes, giving the kind of detail and context that most history shows can't match. His ability to make 18th-century politics gripping is remarkable.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Rest+Is+History+podcast">3. The Rest Is History</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/rest-is-history.jpg" alt="The Rest Is History podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Tom Holland and Dominic Sandbrook cover history like two friends arguing in a pub — knowledgeable, funny and always willing to take a detour. Their military episodes on D-Day, Stalingrad and the Napoleonic Wars are standouts. Consistently one of the UK's most popular podcasts.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Lions+Led+by+Donkeys+podcast">4. Lions Led by Donkeys</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/lions-led-by-donkeys.jpg" alt="Lions Led by Donkeys podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Joe Kassabian looks at military history through the lens of incompetent leadership, disastrous campaigns and the soldiers who paid the price. It's irreverent and darkly funny but thoroughly researched. Episodes on failed coups, pointless battles and delusional generals are consistently excellent.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=War+on+the+Rocks+podcast">5. War on the Rocks</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/war-on-the-rocks.jpg" alt="War on the Rocks podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>More strategic than historical, War on the Rocks features analysis from military professionals, academics and defence policy experts. If you want to understand modern conflict, geopolitics and defence strategy at a serious level, this is where the experts actually talk to each other.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=History+of+the+Second+World+War+podcast">6. History of the Second World War</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/history-second-world-war.jpg" alt="History of the Second World War podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A chronological walk through every major event of World War II, episode by episode. The level of detail is extraordinary — individual battles, political decisions, home front developments and the experiences of ordinary people. If you want to truly understand the war, this is the most thorough podcast available.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+History+of+Rome+podcast+Mike+Duncan">7. The History of Rome</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/history-of-rome.jpg" alt="The History of Rome podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Mike Duncan's first podcast traces Rome from founding myth to the fall of the Western Empire across 179 episodes. The military campaigns — Punic Wars, Caesar's conquests, the crisis of the third century — are told with clarity and momentum. The show that launched a thousand history podcasts.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Real+Dictators+podcast">8. Real Dictators</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-war-military-history-podcasts/real-dictators.jpg" alt="Real Dictators podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Each series profiles a different dictator — how they rose to power, how they held it and what happened when they fell. The military dimensions of figures like Stalin, Saddam Hussein and Idi Amin are explored in detail. Narrated by Paul McGann with high production values throughout.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
