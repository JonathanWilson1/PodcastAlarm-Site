<p>You don't need an app with a subscription to meditate. Some of the best guided meditations and mindfulness teachings are free, right in your podcast player. These 10 shows range from quick daily sits to deep explorations of Buddhist philosophy — pick whatever matches where you are right now.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Ten+Percent+Happier+Dan+Harris">1. Ten Percent Happier</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/ten-percent-happier.jpg" alt="Ten Percent Happier podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dan Harris was a sceptic who had a panic attack on live TV and ended up becoming a meditation evangelist. His interviews with teachers, scientists and authors are grounded, practical and refreshingly free of woo. The best entry point for anyone who thinks meditation isn't for them.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Daily+Meditation+Podcast">2. The Daily Meditation Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/daily-meditation-podcast.jpg" alt="The Daily Meditation Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Mary Meckley delivers a short guided meditation every single day, usually themed around a weekly topic. Episodes run about 10 minutes — just enough to build a daily habit without it feeling like a chore. Perfect for mornings.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Meditation+Minis+Chel+Hamilton">3. Meditation Minis</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/meditation-minis.jpg" alt="Meditation Minis podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Chel Hamilton's short guided meditations are designed for anxiety, sleep and stress relief. Her voice is genuinely calming without being saccharine. Episodes are under 15 minutes and work brilliantly as a reset in the middle of a hectic day.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Tara+Brach+Podcast">4. Tara Brach</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/tara-brach.jpg" alt="Tara Brach podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Tara Brach is a clinical psychologist and meditation teacher whose weekly talks blend Buddhist wisdom with practical psychology. She covers self-compassion, radical acceptance and emotional healing. Her guided meditations at the end of each talk are worth the listen alone.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Untangle+Podcast+meditation">5. Untangle</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/untangle.jpg" alt="Untangle podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Untangle interviews meditation teachers, neuroscientists and psychologists about the science and practice of mindfulness. It's more conversational than most meditation podcasts, which makes it great for people who want to understand why meditation works, not just do it.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Plum+Village+Thich+Nhat+Hanh">6. Plum Village</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/plum-village.jpg" alt="Plum Village podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dharma talks from Thich Nhat Hanh's community in France. These are gentle, profound teachings on mindful living — eating, walking, breathing, being present. If you want something that genuinely slows your brain down, start here.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=On+Being+Krista+Tippett">7. On Being</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/on-being.jpg" alt="On Being podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Krista Tippett's long-running conversations explore the big questions — meaning, faith, ethics, beauty — with poets, scientists, theologians and activists. Not a meditation podcast in the traditional sense, but deeply mindful in every episode. The kind of show that changes how you think.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Nothing+Much+Happens+Kathryn+Nicolai">8. Nothing Much Happens</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/nothing-much-happens.jpg" alt="Nothing Much Happens podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Kathryn Nicolai reads gentle bedtime stories for adults where, true to the name, nothing much happens. A walk through autumn leaves. Making soup. Sitting by a fire. It's designed to help you fall asleep, and it works absurdly well.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Meditation+Mountain+podcast">9. Meditation Mountain</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/meditation-mountain.jpg" alt="Meditation Mountain podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Straightforward guided meditations with a focus on visualisation and relaxation. No lengthy preamble, no philosophy lectures — just press play and follow along. Great for beginners who want something simple and effective.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Mindful+Minute+podcast+meditation">10. Mindful Minute</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-meditation-podcasts/mindful-minute.jpg" alt="Mindful Minute podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Meryl Arnett delivers guided meditations and mindfulness exercises in short, accessible episodes. The show covers everything from body scans to loving-kindness practice. A solid pick if you want variety without long time commitments.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
