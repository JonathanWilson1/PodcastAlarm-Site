<p>Between Artemis missions, private space stations and new telescope data rewriting what we thought we knew about the universe, there has never been a better time to follow space. These 8 podcasts cover everything from astrophysics to mission updates, and they make complex science genuinely enjoyable.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=StarTalk+Radio+Neil+deGrasse+Tyson">1. StarTalk Radio</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/startalk-radio.jpg" alt="StarTalk Radio podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Neil deGrasse Tyson pairs astrophysics with pop culture, comedy and celebrity guests. StarTalk makes big science accessible without dumbing it down, and the mix of comedians and researchers keeps episodes unpredictable. One of the most popular science podcasts for a reason.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Planetary+Radio+Planetary+Society">2. Planetary Radio</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/planetary-radio.jpg" alt="Planetary Radio podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Planetary Society's flagship show interviews the scientists, engineers and astronauts behind current missions. Their coverage of the Artemis program has been excellent, with direct access to the people building the hardware. Essential listening if you want to understand where space exploration is actually heading.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Houston+We+Have+a+Podcast+NASA">3. Houston We Have a Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/houston-we-have-a-podcast.jpg" alt="Houston We Have a Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>NASA's official podcast from Johnson Space Center goes behind the scenes on ISS operations, Artemis mission planning and astronaut training. Hosted by NASA's own communications team, it's the closest you'll get to a seat in mission control. Unfiltered access to the people doing the work.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Infinite+Monkey+Cage+BBC">4. The Infinite Monkey Cage</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/infinite-monkey-cage.jpg" alt="The Infinite Monkey Cage podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Brian Cox and Robin Ince mix physics with comedy in this BBC panel show. Space topics come up frequently — black holes, the search for life, the fate of the universe — and the panel format means you get proper scientific debate alongside genuine laughs. Smart and entertaining in equal measure.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Astronomy+Cast+podcast">5. Astronomy Cast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/astronomy-cast.jpg" alt="Astronomy Cast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Fraser Cain and Dr. Pamela Gay have been methodically working through the universe since 2006. Each episode takes one topic — neutron stars, dark energy, planetary formation — and explains it clearly without scripts or notes. Over 700 episodes and still going strong.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=SpaceTime+with+Stuart+Gary">6. SpaceTime with Stuart Gary</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/spacetime-stuart-gary.jpg" alt="SpaceTime with Stuart Gary podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A weekly news show covering the latest in space science, astronomy and space exploration. Stuart Gary delivers three or four stories per episode with just the right amount of detail. Great for staying current on launches, discoveries and mission updates without wading through press releases.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Daniel+and+Jorge+Explain+the+Universe">7. Daniel and Jorge Explain the Universe</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/daniel-jorge-universe.jpg" alt="Daniel and Jorge Explain the Universe podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Particle physicist Daniel Whiteson and cartoonist Jorge Cham tackle the biggest questions in physics and cosmology. Their episodes on dark matter, the shape of the universe and what happened before the Big Bang are genuinely mind-expanding. Complex ideas explained with humour and patience.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Are+We+There+Yet+space+podcast">8. Are We There Yet?</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-space-astronomy-podcasts/are-we-there-yet.jpg" alt="Are We There Yet? podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Grant Thompson and Andrew Kuntz focus on when humanity will actually reach Mars, build moon bases and become a multi-planetary species. They track Artemis milestones, SpaceX Starship tests and other developments with infectious optimism. Perfect for anyone who wants to know when the future actually arrives.</p>
