<p>Money podcasts used to mean dry stock tips and jargon-heavy analysis. Not any more. The best personal finance shows in 2026 make budgeting, investing and debt payoff genuinely interesting — and they'll teach you more than most financial advisors. Here are 10 worth your time.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Ramsey+Show+Dave+Ramsey">1. The Ramsey Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/ramsey-show.jpg" alt="The Ramsey Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dave Ramsey takes live calls from people in financial trouble and gives them blunt, no-nonsense advice. His baby steps system has helped millions get out of debt. You might not agree with everything he says, but the show is addictive listening.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Planet+Money+NPR">2. Planet Money</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/planet-money.jpg" alt="Planet Money podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>NPR's Planet Money explains the economy through fascinating stories — they once bought a barrel of crude oil just to see what happens to it. Episodes are short, surprising and make complex financial topics feel effortless to understand.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Motley+Fool+Money">3. Motley Fool Money</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/motley-fool-money.jpg" alt="Motley Fool Money podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Motley Fool team breaks down the week's biggest business and investing stories with clarity and a touch of humour. They interview CEOs, analysts and authors, making stock market news accessible to everyone — not just Wall Street insiders.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Money+Guy+Show">4. The Money Guy Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/money-guy-show.jpg" alt="The Money Guy Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Brian Preston and Bo Hanson break down financial decisions by age — what to do in your 20s, 30s, 40s and beyond. Their Financial Order of Operations is one of the clearest frameworks for building wealth you'll find anywhere.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=We+Study+Billionaires+podcast">5. We Study Billionaires</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/we-study-billionaires.jpg" alt="We Study Billionaires podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Investor's Podcast Network flagship show studies the strategies of Warren Buffett, Ray Dalio and other legendary investors. Deep dives into value investing, macroeconomics and wealth building — serious but accessible.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Afford+Anything+Paula+Pant">6. Afford Anything</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/afford-anything.jpg" alt="Afford Anything podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Paula Pant's mantra is simple: you can afford anything, but not everything. Her show covers real estate investing, financial independence and the psychology of money with sharp insight and zero gatekeeping.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Indicator+from+Planet+Money+NPR">7. The Indicator from Planet Money</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/the-indicator.jpg" alt="The Indicator podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Planet Money's daily spin-off takes one economic idea and explains it in under 10 minutes. Perfect for staying informed without committing to a long listen. The hosts make even inflation data feel interesting.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=BiggerPockets+Money+podcast">8. BiggerPockets Money</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/biggerpockets-money.jpg" alt="BiggerPockets Money podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>From the team behind the biggest real estate investing community online. The Money show focuses on personal finance stories — real people sharing exactly how they paid off debt, built savings and reached financial freedom.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=So+Money+Farnoosh+Torabi">9. So Money</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/so-money.jpg" alt="So Money podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Farnoosh Torabi interviews bestselling authors, entrepreneurs and financial experts, then takes listener questions on Fridays. Her style is warm and direct, and she's particularly good on the emotional side of money decisions.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Stacking+Benjamins+podcast">10. Stacking Benjamins</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-personal-finance-podcasts/stacking-benjamins.jpg" alt="Stacking Benjamins podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Joe Saul-Sehy and his team take a lighter approach to money talk — part comedy show, part financial education. Headlines, interviews and listener letters delivered from "Joe's mum's basement." Proof that personal finance doesn't have to be boring.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
