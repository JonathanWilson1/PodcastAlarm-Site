<p>Whether you're a true believer or a devoted sceptic, a good paranormal podcast makes you pause and wonder. These shows cover everything from ghosts and UFOs to cryptids and the genuinely unexplained — and the best ones take the subject matter seriously without losing their sense of fun.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Astonishing+Legends">1. Astonishing Legends</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/astonishing-legends.jpg" alt="Astonishing Legends podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Scott and Forrest go deep on one paranormal topic at a time — sometimes across multiple episodes. Their research is genuinely impressive, and they approach each subject with curiosity rather than credulity. The Dyatlov Pass and Skinwalker Ranch series are outstanding.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Lore+Podcast+Aaron+Mahnke">2. Lore</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/lore.jpg" alt="Lore podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Aaron Mahnke explores the dark folklore and real history behind our fears. Each episode is beautifully produced and narrated, blending true events with the legends they inspired. It spawned a TV show for good reason — the storytelling is that good.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Jim+Harold+Campfire">3. Jim Harold's Campfire</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/jim-harolds-campfire.jpg" alt="Jim Harold's Campfire podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Real people call in to share their own paranormal experiences — ghosts, shadow figures, premonitions, the lot. Jim Harold is a warm, encouraging host who lets his callers tell their stories without judgement. Some of these accounts will genuinely give you chills.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Uncanny+BBC+Danny+Robins">4. Uncanny</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/uncanny.jpg" alt="Uncanny podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Danny Robins investigates real cases of the supernatural reported by ordinary people, bringing in both believers and sceptics to examine the evidence. The BBC production values are superb and Robins strikes the perfect balance between open-minded and rigorous.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Paranormal+Podcast+Jim+Harold">5. The Paranormal Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/the-paranormal-podcast.jpg" alt="The Paranormal Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jim Harold's interview show features authors, researchers and investigators from across the paranormal spectrum. With hundreds of episodes in the archive, it's a massive library of supernatural deep dives. Great for long drives.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Spooked+Snap+Judgment">6. Spooked</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/spooked.jpg" alt="Spooked podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A spin-off from Snap Judgment, Spooked features true-life supernatural stories told by the people who lived them. The production quality is exceptional and the stories are selected for maximum unease. Best listened to in the dark.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Real+Ghost+Stories+Online">7. Real Ghost Stories Online</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/real-ghost-stories-online.jpg" alt="Real Ghost Stories Online podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Listener-submitted ghost stories read and discussed by the hosts. It's straightforward and unpretentious — just people sharing the creepy things that happened to them. The sheer volume of episodes means you'll never run out.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=And+Thats+Why+We+Drink">8. And That's Why We Drink</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/and-thats-why-we-drink.jpg" alt="And That's Why We Drink podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Em and Christine pair a paranormal story with a true crime story each episode, with plenty of banter and wine in between. It's funny, spooky and endlessly listenable. If you want paranormal content that doesn't take itself too seriously, this is your show.</p>
