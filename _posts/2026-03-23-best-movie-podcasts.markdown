<p>If you love films, you need podcasts that match your obsession. Not just reviews — deep dives, director retrospectives, heated debates and the kind of analysis that makes you want to rewatch everything. Here are the 10 best movie podcasts going right now.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Blank+Check+Griffin+David">1. Blank Check</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/blank-check.jpg" alt="Blank Check podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Griffin Newman and David Sims work through a director's entire filmography, one movie per episode. The concept is simple but the conversations are wildly entertaining — funny, nerdy and full of genuine insight. A massive back catalogue to dig into.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Rewatchables+Bill+Simmons">2. The Rewatchables</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/the-rewatchables.jpg" alt="The Rewatchables podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Bill Simmons and the Ringer crew pick apart movies that are endlessly rewatchable — Heat, Goodfellas, The Departed, you name it. They assign awards like "most rewatchable scene" and "Dion Waiters award" and it's exactly as fun as it sounds.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Kermode+and+Mayos+Take">3. Kermode and Mayo's Take</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/kermode-and-mayos-take.jpg" alt="Kermode and Mayo's Take podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Mark Kermode and Simon Mayo are a British institution. Their chemistry is perfect — Kermode delivers passionate, sometimes furious reviews while Mayo keeps things grounded. Nobody reviews films with more conviction or entertainment value.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=How+Did+This+Get+Made">4. How Did This Get Made</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/how-did-this-get-made.jpg" alt="How Did This Get Made podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Paul Scheer, June Diane Raphael and Jason Mantzoukas watch terrible movies and try to figure out how they got greenlit. It's laugh-out-loud funny and surprisingly well-researched. You'll end up watching the bad movies just to keep up.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Big+Picture+Ringer+podcast">5. The Big Picture</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/the-big-picture.jpg" alt="The Big Picture podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Sean Fennessey and Amanda Dobbins cover the film industry with the seriousness it deserves — new releases, Oscar campaigns, industry trends and big-picture analysis. Smart film criticism for people who want more than star ratings.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Filmspotting+podcast">6. Filmspotting</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/filmspotting.jpg" alt="Filmspotting podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Running since 2005, Filmspotting is one of the longest-running film podcasts for good reason. Thoughtful reviews, top-five lists and deep dives into cinema history. It treats film as art without ever being pretentious about it.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=You+Must+Remember+This+Karina+Longworth">7. You Must Remember This</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/you-must-remember-this.jpg" alt="You Must Remember This podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Karina Longworth narrates the secret and forgotten histories of Hollywood's first century. Meticulously researched and beautifully told — each season covers a different era or theme. If you love old Hollywood, this is essential listening.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Evolution+of+Horror+podcast">8. Evolution of Horror</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/evolution-of-horror.jpg" alt="Evolution of Horror podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Mike Muncer traces the history of horror cinema with encyclopaedic knowledge and infectious enthusiasm. Each season covers a subgenre — slashers, folk horror, vampires — and the depth of research is staggering. A must for horror fans.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Pop+Culture+Happy+Hour+NPR">9. Pop Culture Happy Hour</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/pop-culture-happy-hour.jpg" alt="Pop Culture Happy Hour podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>NPR's panel of critics and pop culture obsessives discuss the week's biggest films, TV shows and cultural moments. The tone is warm but the opinions are sharp, and they always make you think differently about what you've just watched. A weekly delight.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Bechdel+Cast+podcast">10. The Bechdel Cast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-movie-podcasts/the-bechdel-cast.jpg" alt="The Bechdel Cast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Caitlin Durante and Jamie Loftus examine movies through an intersectional feminist lens — and it's much funnier than that description suggests. They apply the Bechdel test and beyond to everything from Disney to Die Hard. Sharp, entertaining criticism.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
