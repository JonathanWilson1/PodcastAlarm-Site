<p>From the Iran-Trump standoff to shifting alliances in Asia and Europe, 2026 has been a year where understanding geopolitics actually matters for everyday life. These 8 podcasts break down global power dynamics without assuming you have a degree in international relations.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Rest+Is+Politics">1. The Rest Is Politics</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/rest-is-politics.jpg" alt="The Rest Is Politics podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Alastair Campbell and Rory Stewart bring left and right perspectives to the biggest political stories of the week. Their chemistry is genuine and they disagree respectfully, which feels increasingly rare. The show has expanded into a global edition covering US and world politics too.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Pod+Save+the+World">2. Pod Save the World</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/pod-save-the-world.jpg" alt="Pod Save the World podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Tommy Vietor, a former National Security Council spokesperson, interviews diplomats, journalists and policy experts about foreign affairs. His insider perspective makes the analysis sharper than most. Recent episodes on the Iran-Trump tensions and their ripple effects across the Middle East have been particularly timely.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=War+on+the+Rocks+podcast">3. War on the Rocks</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/war-on-the-rocks.jpg" alt="War on the Rocks podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A national security and defence podcast that goes deeper than the headlines. The guests are military strategists, intelligence analysts and foreign policy scholars who bring real expertise. Dense but rewarding — the kind of show that helps you understand why certain conflicts escalate and others do not.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Power+Corrupts+Brian+Klaas">4. Power Corrupts</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/power-corrupts.jpg" alt="Power Corrupts podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Political scientist Brian Klaas examines how power works, who seeks it and why it corrupts. The show blends history with current events — an episode about authoritarian playbooks feels uncomfortably relevant when you read the next day's headlines about democratic backsliding worldwide.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Worldly+Vox+podcast">5. Worldly</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/worldly.jpg" alt="Worldly podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Vox's foreign policy podcast breaks down international events for a general audience. The hosts explain why a conflict in one region ripples through trade, migration and security elsewhere. Their recent episodes on US-Iran diplomacy and Trump-era foreign policy shifts have been particularly sharp.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Intelligence+Squared+podcast">6. Intelligence Squared</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/intelligence-squared.jpg" alt="Intelligence Squared podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Long-form debates and interviews with world leaders, diplomats and thinkers on the issues shaping global politics. The debate format forces both sides to make their strongest case, which is far more useful than the usual pundit echo chamber. Consistently excellent guests.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Global+News+Podcast+BBC">7. Global News Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/global-news-podcast.jpg" alt="Global News Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The BBC World Service's flagship podcast delivers twice-daily international news with correspondents on the ground worldwide. It is the fastest way to understand what is happening globally — 30 minutes covers more geopolitical ground than most shows manage in an hour.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Foreign+Policy+Playlist+podcast">8. Foreign Policy Playlist</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-geopolitics-podcasts/foreign-policy-playlist.jpg" alt="Foreign Policy Playlist podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Foreign Policy magazine curates their best audio reporting into a weekly playlist format. Episodes cover sanctions, alliances, energy politics and the diplomatic manoeuvring behind the headlines. A good way to stay broadly informed without committing to a dozen different feeds.</p>
