<p>Fiction podcasts have quietly become one of the best storytelling mediums going. Full casts, sound design, original scores — the best ones rival anything on TV. These 10 shows prove that audio drama is having a proper golden age.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Welcome+to+Night+Vale">1. Welcome to Night Vale</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/welcome-to-night-vale.jpg" alt="Welcome to Night Vale podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Community radio updates from a desert town where every conspiracy theory is true. Cecil Palmer reports on the mundane and the terrifying with the same calm tone. It's weird, funny, creepy and oddly comforting — and it basically launched the fiction podcast genre.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Magnus+Archives">2. The Magnus Archives</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/the-magnus-archives.jpg" alt="The Magnus Archives podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jonathan Sims reads statements of supernatural encounters from the archives of the Magnus Institute. Starts as monster-of-the-week horror, then slowly reveals a vast interconnected mythology. 200 episodes, now complete, and genuinely one of the best horror stories told in any medium.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Black+Tapes+Podcast">3. The Black Tapes</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/the-black-tapes.jpg" alt="The Black Tapes podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A journalist investigates a paranormal research institute and its enigmatic founder, Dr Richard Strand. Presented as a serialised docuseries, it blurs the line between fiction and reality brilliantly. The mystery deepens with every episode and the atmosphere is genuinely unsettling.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Limetown+podcast">4. Limetown</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/limetown.jpg" alt="Limetown podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A journalist investigates the disappearance of over 300 people from a neuroscience research facility in Tennessee. Presented as an investigative report, it feels unnervingly real. Season one is tight, gripping and over far too quickly.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Bright+Sessions+podcast">5. The Bright Sessions</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/the-bright-sessions.jpg" alt="The Bright Sessions podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Therapy sessions with people who have supernatural abilities — a time traveller with anxiety, an empath who can't cope with crowds. It's a clever concept that uses superpowers as metaphors for real mental health struggles. Warm, thoughtful and surprisingly emotional.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Penumbra+Podcast">6. The Penumbra Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/the-penumbra-podcast.jpg" alt="The Penumbra Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A genre-hopping anthology featuring Juno Steel, a private eye on Mars, alongside tales of myth, romance and adventure. The voice acting is superb, the writing is sharp, and the show features brilliantly diverse characters. Equal parts noir, sci-fi and fairy tale.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Old+Gods+of+Appalachia">7. Old Gods of Appalachia</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/old-gods-of-appalachia.jpg" alt="Old Gods of Appalachia podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dark folklore horror set in the mountains of Appalachia, where ancient things sleep beneath the hills. Steve Shell narrates with a campfire storyteller's cadence that gets under your skin. The sound design and original music are phenomenal.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+White+Vault+podcast">8. The White Vault</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/the-white-vault.jpg" alt="The White Vault podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A repair team is sent to a remote Arctic research station and discovers something ancient beneath the ice. Told through found audio recordings in multiple languages, it builds dread slowly and deliberately. Cosmic horror fans, this one's for you.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Adventure+Zone">9. The Adventure Zone</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/the-adventure-zone.jpg" alt="The Adventure Zone podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The McElroy brothers and their dad play tabletop RPGs and somehow create one of the most emotionally affecting fiction podcasts ever made. The Balance arc is legendary — funny, inventive and genuinely moving. It proves that improv storytelling can rival any scripted drama.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Archive+81+podcast">10. Archive 81</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-fiction-podcasts/archive-81.jpg" alt="Archive 81 podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>An archivist restores damaged audio tapes and gets pulled into a story involving a strange apartment building, ritual magic and cosmic horror. The found-footage format works perfectly in audio. Netflix adapted it, but the podcast goes to much weirder places.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
