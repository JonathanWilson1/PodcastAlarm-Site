<p>Relationships are hard, and most of us never learned how to do them well. Whether you are working through something specific or just want to understand people better, these 8 podcasts offer honest, practical insight from therapists, couples and people who have been through it.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Where+Should+We+Begin+Esther+Perel">1. Where Should We Begin? with Esther Perel</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/where-should-we-begin.jpg" alt="Where Should We Begin podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Esther Perel records real therapy sessions with anonymous couples and lets you listen in. It is raw, uncomfortable and utterly compelling. You will recognise your own relationship patterns in nearly every episode, and that is exactly the point.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Savage+Lovecast+Dan+Savage">2. Savage Lovecast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/savage-lovecast.jpg" alt="Savage Lovecast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dan Savage has been giving relationship and sex advice for decades, and his call-in podcast is as sharp as ever. He is direct, funny and genuinely wise about the messy realities of love and desire. If you want advice without sugarcoating, this is your show.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Love+Drive+podcast">3. The Love Drive</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/the-love-drive.jpg" alt="The Love Drive podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Shaun Galanos interviews relationship experts, sex educators and therapists about love, connection and communication. The conversations go deep without getting academic. A great pick if you want to understand the mechanics of healthy relationships.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Dear+Therapists+Lori+Gottlieb">4. Dear Therapists</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/dear-therapists.jpg" alt="Dear Therapists podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Lori Gottlieb and Guy Winch take letters from real people struggling with relationship problems and work through them together. It is like advice column meets therapy session. Lori's book "Maybe You Should Talk to Someone" fans will love this.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Relationship+Alive+Neil+Sattin">5. Relationship Alive</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/relationship-alive.jpg" alt="Relationship Alive podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Neil Sattin interviews leading researchers and therapists including John Gottman, Stan Tatkin and Sue Johnson. The show digs into attachment theory, communication skills and conflict resolution with real depth. Over 300 episodes, so there is something for every relationship challenge.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Love+Is+Like+a+Plant+podcast">6. Love Is Like a Plant</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/love-is-like-a-plant.jpg" alt="Love Is Like a Plant podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ellen Huerta and Sarah May B share honest conversations about breakups, healing and learning to love again. The show started as a breakup podcast but has grown into something broader about emotional growth. It feels like talking to friends who actually understand.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=U+Up+podcast+Betches">7. U Up?</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/u-up.jpg" alt="U Up? podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jordana Abraham and Jared Freid break down modern dating with humour and honesty. They answer listener questions about texting, ghosting, situationships and everything else that makes dating in 2026 feel impossible. Light enough to be fun, real enough to actually help.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Multiamory+podcast">8. Multiamory</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-relationship-podcasts/multiamory.jpg" alt="Multiamory podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Multiamory covers communication, boundaries and relationship skills through the lens of non-monogamy, but the advice is genuinely useful for anyone in any kind of relationship. The hosts are thoughtful, research-backed and refreshingly non-judgmental about how people structure their love lives.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
