<p>Podcasts are one of the best ways to learn Spanish because they train your ear for real conversation — not just textbook phrases. These 10 shows cover everything from absolute beginner lessons to full-speed native content. Pick the ones that match your level and listen daily.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Coffee+Break+Spanish">1. Coffee Break Spanish</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/coffee-break-spanish.jpg" alt="Coffee Break Spanish podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Mark and his team at Radio Lingua build your Spanish from scratch with structured, well-paced lessons. The format works brilliantly for beginners — each episode introduces new vocabulary and grammar without overwhelming you. One of the longest-running and most trusted language podcasts around.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=SpanishPod101">2. SpanishPod101</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/spanishpod101.jpg" alt="SpanishPod101 podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A massive library of lessons organised by level, from absolute beginner to advanced. The hosts break down dialogues line by line, explaining grammar and cultural context as they go. The sheer volume of content means you'll never run out of material.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Notes+in+Spanish">3. Notes in Spanish</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/notes-in-spanish.jpg" alt="Notes in Spanish podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ben and Marina — he's British, she's Spanish — have natural conversations that gradually increase in difficulty. The chemistry between them is genuine, which makes the listening feel less like a lesson and more like eavesdropping on a couple chatting over dinner in Madrid.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Duolingo+Spanish+Podcast">4. Duolingo Spanish Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/duolingo-spanish.jpg" alt="Duolingo Spanish Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>True stories told in easy-to-follow Spanish with English narration to keep you on track. The storytelling is genuinely compelling — you'll forget you're learning because you're too busy wanting to know what happens next. Perfect for intermediate learners.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=News+in+Slow+Spanish">5. News in Slow Spanish</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/news-in-slow-spanish.jpg" alt="News in Slow Spanish podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Real news stories delivered at a slower pace so intermediate learners can follow along. The hosts discuss current events, grammar points and expressions naturally within each episode. A brilliant bridge between beginner lessons and full-speed native content.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Espanolistos+Spanish+Podcast">6. Espanolistos</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/espanolistos.jpg" alt="Espanolistos podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Andrea (Colombian) and Nate (American) discuss everyday topics in a mix of Spanish and English. Their bilingual format is ideal for intermediate learners who want to start thinking in Spanish without getting completely lost. The cultural insights are a bonus.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=How+to+Spanish+Podcast">7. How to Spanish Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/how-to-spanish.jpg" alt="How to Spanish Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A husband-and-wife team breaks down tricky Spanish grammar, slang and pronunciation with clear explanations. They focus on the stuff textbooks skip — regional differences, common mistakes and the phrases you actually need in real conversations.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Unlimited+Spanish+Podcast">8. Unlimited Spanish</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/unlimited-spanish.jpg" alt="Unlimited Spanish podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Oscar uses a point-of-view storytelling technique where he tells the same story from different perspectives and tenses. It sounds simple, but it's remarkably effective at drilling grammar patterns into your brain without boring repetition. Great for intermediate learners who feel stuck.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=No+Hay+Tos+Spanish+Podcast">9. No Hay Tos</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/no-hay-tos.jpg" alt="No Hay Tos podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Hector and Marco teach Mexican Spanish with humour and a focus on slang, idioms and real-world usage. If you're learning Latin American Spanish specifically, this is one of the best resources out there. The energy is infectious and the content is genuinely useful.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Hoy+Hablamos+Spanish+Podcast">10. Hoy Hablamos</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-learning-spanish/hoy-hablamos.jpg" alt="Hoy Hablamos podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A daily podcast entirely in Spanish, aimed at intermediate and advanced learners. Each short episode covers a different topic with clear, measured speech. The daily format means consistent exposure, which is the single most important factor in actually becoming fluent.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
