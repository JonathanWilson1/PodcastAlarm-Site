<p>Some cases never get closed, and something about that keeps pulling us back. These 8 podcasts dig into disappearances, cold cases and unsolved crimes with the kind of detail that keeps you thinking long after the episode ends.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Crime+Junkie+Ashley+Flowers">1. Crime Junkie</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/crime-junkie.jpg" alt="Crime Junkie podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ashley Flowers and Brit Prawat deliver weekly true crime stories with tight pacing and clear storytelling. Their unsolved episodes are some of the best — they lay out the evidence, flag the gaps, and leave you genuinely wanting answers. One of the most popular true crime podcasts for good reason.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Trail+Went+Cold+podcast">2. The Trail Went Cold</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/trail-went-cold.jpg" alt="The Trail Went Cold podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Robin Warder focuses on lesser-known cold cases that deserve more attention. Episodes run about 30 minutes, which makes them easy to fit into a commute. His research is thorough and he presents multiple theories without pushing you toward one conclusion.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=My+Favorite+Murder+podcast">3. My Favorite Murder</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/my-favorite-murder.jpg" alt="My Favorite Murder podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark built a true crime empire by being funny, honest and deeply human about dark subjects. The show covers both solved and unsolved cases, but their unsolved episodes hit differently because they genuinely care about the victims. The "murderino" community is massive for a reason.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Thin+Air+Podcast">4. Thin Air Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/thin-air.jpg" alt="Thin Air Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Thin Air focuses exclusively on missing persons cases. The hosts treat every case with genuine empathy and often speak directly with families of the missing. It fills a gap that most true crime shows overlook — the people who vanished and were never found.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Vanished+Podcast">5. The Vanished Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/the-vanished.jpg" alt="The Vanished Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Marissa Jones has been covering missing persons cases since 2016, often working directly with families to bring attention to cases the media has ignored. The show has actually helped generate tips that led to breaks in real investigations.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=True+Crime+Garage+podcast">6. True Crime Garage</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/true-crime-garage.jpg" alt="True Crime Garage podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Nic and the Captain crack open a beer and work through a case over two episodes. The format gives them room to really dig into the evidence and timelines. Their tone strikes a good balance between casual and respectful — it feels like sitting in with friends who take the cases seriously.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Already+Gone+Podcast">7. Already Gone</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/already-gone.jpg" alt="Already Gone podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Nina Innsted covers cold cases and unsolved murders primarily from the Great Lakes region of the US. Her solo narration style is calm and factual, letting the details of each case speak for themselves. A hidden gem in the true crime space.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Trace+Evidence+podcast">8. Trace Evidence</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-unsolved-mystery-podcasts/trace-evidence.jpg" alt="Trace Evidence podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Steven Pacheco examines unsolved cases with a focus on evidence analysis and logical deduction. He walks through timelines, witness statements and physical evidence methodically. Each episode ends with his own theory, which is always well-reasoned even when the case seems impossible.</p>
