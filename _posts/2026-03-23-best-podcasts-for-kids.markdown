<p>Podcasts are one of the best screen-free ways to keep kids entertained and learning. Whether you need something for a long car journey or just want an alternative to YouTube, these 10 shows are genuinely good — entertaining enough that kids actually ask to listen again.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Little+Stories+for+Tiny+People">1. Little Stories for Tiny People</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/little-stories-tiny-people.jpg" alt="Little Stories for Tiny People podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Rhea Pechter writes and narrates original short stories for young children that are gentle, imaginative and perfect for bedtime or quiet time. Each tale features kind characters and simple adventures that hold little ones' attention without overstimulating them.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=But+Why+A+Podcast+for+Curious+Kids">2. But Why: A Podcast for Curious Kids</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/but-why.jpg" alt="But Why podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Kids ask questions and real experts answer them. Why is the sky blue? Why do dogs have tails? Host Jane Lindholm takes every question seriously and delivers answers that are clear, accurate and never condescending.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Brains+On+podcast">3. Brains On!</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/brains-on.jpg" alt="Brains On podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A science podcast co-hosted by a different kid each week alongside journalist Molly Bloom. Covers everything from black holes to belly buttons with proper research and fantastic sound design. Think Radiolab for under-12s.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Story+Pirates+podcast">4. Story Pirates</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/story-pirates.jpg" alt="Story Pirates podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Kids submit stories and a troupe of professional actors and comedians bring them to life with sketches, songs and wild improvisation. The results are unpredictable, hilarious and genuinely creative. A favourite for ages 4-10.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Circle+Round+WBUR+podcast">5. Circle Round</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/circle-round.jpg" alt="Circle Round podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>WBUR adapts folk tales from around the world into beautifully produced audio dramas with professional voice actors and original music. Each episode carries a gentle moral without being preachy. Perfect for bedtime or quiet time.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Smash+Boom+Best+podcast">6. Smash Boom Best</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/smash-boom-best.jpg" alt="Smash Boom Best podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A debate show for kids from the Brains On team. Two things go head to head — unicorns vs dragons, pizza vs tacos — and champions argue their case. It teaches critical thinking and persuasion while being completely entertaining.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Tumble+Science+Podcast+for+Kids">7. Tumble Science Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/tumble-science.jpg" alt="Tumble Science Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Married science journalist Lindsay Patterson and teacher Marshall Escamilla explore a different science story each episode. The conversations feel natural and curious rather than educational, which is exactly why kids stay engaged.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Past+and+The+Curious+podcast">8. The Past and The Curious</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/past-and-curious.jpg" alt="The Past and The Curious podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Mick Sullivan tells weird, wonderful and mostly true stories from history that kids (and adults) have never heard before. The tone is warm and funny, and the stories are genuinely surprising. Great for long car journeys.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Greeking+Out+National+Geographic+Kids">9. Greeking Out</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/greeking-out.jpg" alt="Greeking Out podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>National Geographic Kids retells Greek myths with energy, humour and sound effects that bring each story to life. Zeus, Athena, Heracles — they all show up, and the stories never feel stale. Mythology made genuinely fun.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Who+Smarted+podcast">10. Who Smarted?</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-kids/who-smarted.jpg" alt="Who Smarted podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Host Trusty the Narrator takes kids on adventures through history, science and pop culture in short, quiz-style episodes. Each one packs in real facts with comedy and keeps episodes tight enough for short attention spans.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
