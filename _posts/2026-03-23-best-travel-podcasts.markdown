<p>Travel podcasts are at their best when they make you want to book a flight before the episode ends. Whether you're planning your next trip or just want to escape your commute for 30 minutes, these 10 shows cover everything from budget backpacking to armchair exploration.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Zero+To+Travel+podcast">1. Zero To Travel</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/zero-to-travel.jpg" alt="Zero To Travel podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jason Moore interviews long-term travellers, digital nomads and location-independent workers about how they actually make it work. Practical, actionable and full of ideas for people who want to travel more but don't know where to start. One of the originals in the space.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Untold+Italy+podcast">2. Untold Italy</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/untold-italy.jpg" alt="Untold Italy podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Katy Clarke dives deep into Italian regions, food traditions and hidden gems that most travel guides skip. Whether you're planning a trip or just dreaming of one, episodes on everything from Puglia's trulli houses to Rome's best street food will leave you reaching for your passport.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Travel+Diaries+Holly+Rubenstein">3. Travel Diaries</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/travel-diaries.jpg" alt="Travel Diaries podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Holly Rubenstein asks celebrities and travel experts about the journeys that shaped them. Guests share their first trip, their favourite place and the destination they'd recommend to anyone. Short, warm episodes that always leave you with somewhere new to add to the list.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Amateur+Traveler+podcast">4. Amateur Traveler</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/amateur-traveler.jpg" alt="Amateur Traveler podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Chris Christensen has been making this show since 2005, covering one destination per episode with a guest who knows it well. Over 800 episodes means there's probably one for wherever you're going. Detailed, practical and consistently useful for actual trip planning.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Extra+Pack+of+Peanuts+podcast">5. Extra Pack of Peanuts</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/extra-pack-of-peanuts.jpg" alt="Extra Pack of Peanuts podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Travis Sherry focuses on budget travel — points hacking, cheap flights, free accommodation and how to see the world without going broke. If you've ever looked at a flight price and given up, this show will change your approach entirely.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Women+Who+Travel+Conde+Nast">6. Women Who Travel</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/women-who-travel.jpg" alt="Women Who Travel podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Conde Nast Traveler's podcast features honest conversations about the joys and challenges of travelling as a woman. Destination guides, solo travel tips and cultural deep dives delivered with warmth and authority. The community around this show is one of the most engaged in travel podcasting.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Thoughtful+Travel+Podcast">7. The Thoughtful Travel Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/thoughtful-travel-podcast.jpg" alt="The Thoughtful Travel Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Amanda Kendle picks a travel theme each week — getting lost, food disasters, unexpected kindness — and invites travellers to share their stories around it. It's less about where to go and more about what travel does to you. Thoughtful is exactly the right word.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Travel+with+Rick+Steves">8. Travel with Rick Steves</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/travel-with-rick-steves.jpg" alt="Travel with Rick Steves podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Rick Steves has been helping Americans travel to Europe for 40 years, and his podcast brings the same practical, culturally curious approach. Interviews with local guides, historians and fellow travellers. Especially strong on European destinations and doing them on a budget.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Nomad+Together+podcast">9. Nomad Together</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/nomad-together.jpg" alt="Nomad Together podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Aimed at families who want to travel long-term or live abroad. Covers the logistics most travel podcasts ignore — schooling, healthcare, visas, keeping kids entertained on 14-hour flights. If you've ever wondered whether you could take the family on the road, start here.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Trip+podcast+travel">10. The Trip</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-travel-podcasts/the-trip.jpg" alt="The Trip podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A narrative travel podcast where each episode follows one journey from start to finish. The stories range from hilarious misadventures to genuinely moving encounters with strangers. It captures what travel actually feels like — messy, surprising and full of moments you didn't plan for.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
