<p>The best weight loss podcasts skip the gimmicks and focus on what actually works — habit change, honest talk and real science. These 10 shows are hosted by people who've done it themselves or spent careers helping others. No magic pills, just good advice.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Half+Size+Me+Heather+Robertson">1. Half Size Me</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/half-size-me.jpg" alt="Half Size Me podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Heather Robertson lost over 170 pounds and has kept it off for years. Her podcast interviews people who've done the same — no fad diets, just sustainable habits and real stories from people living it every day.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Chalene+Show">2. The Chalene Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/the-chalene-show.jpg" alt="The Chalene Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Chalene Johnson covers diet, fitness and lifestyle balance with infectious energy and a no-nonsense approach. She's been in the fitness industry for decades and cuts through fad diets with practical, sustainable advice that actually fits into a busy life.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Model+Health+Show+Shawn+Stevenson">3. The Model Health Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/the-model-health-show.jpg" alt="The Model Health Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Shawn Stevenson covers nutrition, exercise and sleep with a research-backed approach that never feels dry. He interviews top experts and breaks down complex science into things you can actually act on. One of the most popular health podcasts for good reason.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Mind+Your+Body+Podcast">4. Mind Your Body Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/mind-your-body.jpg" alt="Mind Your Body Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>This show tackles the psychological side of weight loss — emotional eating, body image, self-sabotage. If you know what to eat but can't seem to stick with it, the mental game is where the real work happens. This podcast gets that.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Cut+The+Fat+Podcast">5. Cut The Fat Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/cut-the-fat.jpg" alt="Cut The Fat Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Straightforward, evidence-based advice on fat loss without the nonsense. Episodes cover specific topics like meal timing, exercise selection and plateau-busting. Short enough to listen to during a workout, useful enough to change your approach.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Diet+Starts+Tomorrow">6. Diet Starts Tomorrow</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/diet-starts-tomorrow.jpg" alt="Diet Starts Tomorrow podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A refreshingly honest take on diet culture, body image and the emotional side of weight loss. The hosts tackle the contradictions we all feel about food and fitness without judgement. If you've ever started a diet on Monday and abandoned it by Wednesday, you'll feel seen.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Found+My+Fitness+Rhonda+Patrick">7. Found My Fitness</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/found-my-fitness.jpg" alt="Found My Fitness podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dr. Rhonda Patrick goes deep on the science of nutrition, metabolism and longevity. Not a weight loss show per se, but the research she covers will fundamentally change how you think about food, exercise and your body. For the science-minded listener.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Lose+Weight+for+the+Last+Time">8. Lose Weight for the Last Time</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/lose-weight-last-time.jpg" alt="Lose Weight for the Last Time podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dr Jill Cruz focuses on the mindset and habits behind lasting weight loss. Her episodes are compassionate but direct — she understands why smart people struggle with food and gives evidence-based tools to break the cycle for good.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Fitness+Confidential+Vinnie+Tortorich">9. Fitness Confidential</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/fitness-confidential.jpg" alt="Fitness Confidential podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Celebrity trainer Vinnie Tortorich pulls no punches on the fitness and diet industry. His NSNG (No Sugar No Grains) approach is simple and his rants about misleading health marketing are both entertaining and educational. Opinionated but well-informed.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Nutrition+Diva+Monica+Reinagel">10. The Nutrition Diva</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-weight-loss-podcasts/the-nutrition-diva.jpg" alt="The Nutrition Diva podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Monica Reinagel is a licensed nutritionist who answers common food questions in short, clear episodes. Should you count calories? Is intermittent fasting worth it? She cuts through the noise with actual science in under 10 minutes.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
