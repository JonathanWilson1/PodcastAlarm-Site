<p>Some podcasts help you think. Others help you stop thinking so you can actually get work done. Whether you need ambient sound, productivity systems or a gentle voice to settle your brain before a study session, these 10 shows are built for focus.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Nothing+Much+Happens+Kathryn+Nicolai">1. Nothing Much Happens</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/nothing-much-happens.jpg" alt="Nothing Much Happens podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Kathryn Nicolai reads soothing bedtime stories where, true to the name, nothing much happens. But it works brilliantly as a pre-study wind-down — her calm voice clears the mental clutter so you can actually concentrate. Perfect for resetting a scattered brain.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Productivity+Show+Asian+Efficiency">2. The Productivity Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/the-productivity-show.jpg" alt="The Productivity Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Asian Efficiency's podcast covers time management, study techniques and productivity systems in practical, actionable episodes. No vague motivational waffle — just specific tools and methods you can use immediately. Listen on a break, then apply what you learned.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Deep+Energy+Podcast">3. Deep Energy Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/deep-energy-podcast.jpg" alt="Deep Energy Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jim Butler creates long-form ambient music designed for deep relaxation and focus. No words, no distractions — just gentle soundscapes that fill the silence without competing for your attention. Ideal background audio for long study sessions.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Focus+Session+Podcast">4. The Focus Session Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/the-focus-session-podcast.jpg" alt="The Focus Session Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Short episodes built around timed focus sessions with ambient sound and gentle prompts. Think of it as a podcast-shaped Pomodoro timer. Hit play, do the work, and let the episode structure your session for you.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Study+Music+Podcast">5. Study Music Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/study-music-podcast.jpg" alt="Study Music Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Exactly what it sounds like — curated instrumental music designed to keep you in the zone. Lo-fi, classical, electronic and ambient tracks that fade into the background. No ads, no chatter, just study-friendly sound.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Deep+Questions+Cal+Newport">6. Deep Questions with Cal Newport</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/deep-work.jpg" alt="Deep Questions with Cal Newport podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Cal Newport literally wrote the book on deep work and his podcast applies those principles to real life. He answers listener questions about focus, productivity and building a meaningful career. If your study problem is actually a distraction problem, start here.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Before+Breakfast+Laura+Vanderkam">7. Before Breakfast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/before-breakfast.jpg" alt="Before Breakfast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Laura Vanderkam delivers short, sharp episodes on time management and productivity that you can listen to in under 10 minutes. Practical tips for making the most of your mornings, planning your week and finding time for what matters. Perfect pre-study motivation.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Brain+Science+podcast">8. Brain Science</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/brain-science.jpg" alt="Brain Science podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dr. Ginger Campbell explores neuroscience research in accessible, fascinating episodes. Understanding how your brain actually learns makes you a better student. Listen to this one on a walk, then hit the books with new insight.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Ken+Coleman+Show">9. The Ken Coleman Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/the-ken-coleman-show.jpg" alt="The Ken Coleman Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ken Coleman helps people figure out what they should be studying in the first place. His career-focused advice connects education to purpose — useful when motivation dips and you need reminding why you're putting in the hours.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Deep+Dive+Ali+Abdaal">10. Deep Dive with Ali Abdaal</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-for-studying/ali-abdaal.jpg" alt="Deep Dive with Ali Abdaal podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ali Abdaal went from Cambridge medical student to one of the biggest productivity creators online. His podcast interviews authors, entrepreneurs and scientists about the systems behind high performance. Evidence-based study tips from someone who's genuinely tested them all.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
