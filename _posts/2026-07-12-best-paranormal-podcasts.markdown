These 18 shows cover real hauntings, ghost encounters, cryptids, UFOs and the genuinely unexplained — and the best of them take the subject seriously without losing their sense of fun. They're grouped by what you're actually in the mood for, from first-hand ghost stories to UAP disclosure reporting, and every entry names the episode to start on.</p>

<h2>What's new for 2026</h2>
<p>Three additions this time: <strong>MrBallen</strong>, now one of the biggest strange-and-unexplained shows in the world; <strong>Ghost Town</strong>, for haunted-location history; and <strong>Strange Arrivals</strong>, which takes the abduction phenomenon apart with real journalistic care. We've also taken in the real-encounter shows that used to sit on our horror list — Uncanny, Spooked and Astonishing Legends — so everything grounded in genuine testimony now lives on one page.</p>

<h2>Best ghost podcasts and real haunting stories</h2>
<p>First-hand encounters, told by the people they happened to. If you searched for ghost podcasts, these are the six to try first.</p>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Uncanny+BBC+Danny+Robins">1. Uncanny</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/uncanny.jpg" alt="Uncanny podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Danny Robins investigates real supernatural cases reported by ordinary people, bringing in a believer and a sceptic to pick apart the evidence. The BBC production values are superb, and Robins strikes the perfect balance between open-minded and rigorous. The best-made paranormal show going.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> "Case 1: The Witch Farm" — the case that made the show's name.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Jim+Harold+Campfire">2. Jim Harold's Campfire</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/jim-harolds-campfire.jpg" alt="Jim Harold's Campfire podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Real people call in to share their own paranormal experiences — ghosts, shadow figures, premonitions, the lot. Jim Harold is a warm, encouraging host who lets callers tell their stories without judgement. Some of these accounts will genuinely give you chills.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any Halloween special — they're the strongest story selections of the year.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Spooked+Snap+Judgment">3. Spooked</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/spooked.jpg" alt="Spooked podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A spin-off from Snap Judgment, hosted by Glynn Washington, featuring true supernatural stories told by the people who lived them. These aren't actors — they're ordinary people describing something they can't explain, often with audible unease. The production is cinematic. Best listened to in the dark.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> "The Bunk Bed" — still the episode listeners bring up first.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Real+Ghost+Stories+Online">4. Real Ghost Stories Online</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/real-ghost-stories-online.jpg" alt="Real Ghost Stories Online podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Listener-submitted ghost stories read and discussed by the hosts. Straightforward and unpretentious — just people sharing the creepy things that happened to them. It publishes almost daily, so the archive is effectively bottomless.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any recent episode; there's no running order to catch up on.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Homespun+Haints">5. Homespun Haints</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/homespun-haints.jpg" alt="Homespun Haints podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Diana and Becky collect first-hand ghost stories from ordinary people, on the premise that everybody has a haunting in them somewhere. Warm, conversational and Southern-gothic in flavour, and the variety of encounters — from friendly house spirits to genuinely menacing ones — keeps every episode fresh.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> season 1, episode 1 — the hosts' own family hauntings set the tone.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Ghost+Town+Jason+Horton+Rebecca+Leib">6. Ghost Town</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/ghost-town.jpg" alt="Ghost Town podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jason Horton and Rebecca Leib take one haunted location per episode and tell you the actual history first — who died there, what was covered up, why the story stuck. The research does the heavy lifting, which makes the haunting part land much better than the usual "and then we heard a noise" approach.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> their Cecil Hotel episodes — the definitive audio telling.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h2>Best supernatural podcasts and unexplained deep dives</h2>
<p>Longer-form investigation into the supernatural, the occult and the genuinely inexplicable — for when a single ghost story isn't enough.</p>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Astonishing+Legends">7. Astonishing Legends</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/astonishing-legends.jpg" alt="Astonishing Legends podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Scott Philbrook and Forrest Burgess go deep on one paranormal topic at a time, sometimes across multiple episodes. Their research is genuinely impressive, and they approach each subject with curiosity rather than credulity. Over 300 episodes and still finding fresh territory.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Dyatlov Pass series — the best entry point, and the best thing they've made.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Lore+Podcast+Aaron+Mahnke">8. Lore</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/lore.jpg" alt="Lore podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Aaron Mahnke explores the dark folklore and real history behind our fears. Each episode is beautifully produced and narrated, blending true events with the legends they inspired. It spawned a TV show for good reason — the storytelling is that good.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> episode 1, "They Made a Tonic" — 19th-century New England's vampire panic.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Otherworld+Jack+Wagner">9. Otherworld</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/otherworld.jpg" alt="Otherworld podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jack Wagner interviews people who've had genuinely inexplicable experiences — glitches in reality, shadow people, alternate dimensions, missing time. The sound design is cinematic and Wagner is a disarmingly gentle interviewer, which makes the stranger accounts land even harder. One of the most talked-about paranormal shows in the US, for good reason.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> "The Hat Man" — the episode that turned the show into a phenomenon.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=MrBallen+Strange+Dark+Mysterious">10. MrBallen Podcast: Strange, Dark &amp; Mysterious Stories</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/mrballen-podcast.jpg" alt="MrBallen Podcast Strange Dark and Mysterious Stories podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Former Navy SEAL John Allen tells one strange story per episode — disappearances, hauntings, survival horror, things nobody has satisfactorily explained. His delivery is the whole trick: calm, specific, and he never oversells the ending. The biggest show in this corner of podcasting, and an easy first listen.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode — every one is a self-contained story of about half an hour.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Last+Podcast+on+the+Left">11. Last Podcast on the Left</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/last-podcast-on-the-left.jpg" alt="Last Podcast on the Left podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Marcus Parks, Henry Zebrowski and Ed Larson cover cryptids, hauntings, cults, aliens and the occult with exhaustive research and pitch-black comedy. Irreverent and not for the easily offended, but the deep dives on Bigfoot, the Mothman and UFO lore are as informative as they are funny.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Mothman series — research and stupidity in perfect balance.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=The+Paranormal+Podcast+Jim+Harold">12. The Paranormal Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/the-paranormal-podcast.jpg" alt="The Paranormal Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jim Harold's interview show features authors, researchers and investigators from across the paranormal spectrum. With hundreds of episodes in the archive it's a massive library of supernatural deep dives, and the guest list is the best in the field. Great for long drives.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode whose guest wrote a book you've heard of — that's the show's sweet spot.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=And+Thats+Why+We+Drink">13. And That's Why We Drink</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/and-thats-why-we-drink.jpg" alt="And That's Why We Drink podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Em and Christine pair a paranormal story with a true crime story each episode, with plenty of banter and wine in between. Funny, spooky and endlessly listenable. If you want paranormal content that doesn't take itself too seriously, this is your show.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> episode 1 — the hosts' chemistry is the draw, so start where it starts.</p>

<h2>Best UFO podcasts</h2>
<p>UAP sightings, whistleblower testimony and the disclosure story, covered by people who actually follow the beat.</p>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Somewhere+in+the+Skies+Ryan+Sprague">14. Somewhere in the Skies</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/somewhere-in-the-skies.jpg" alt="Somewhere in the Skies podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ryan Sprague is a serious UFO researcher who's testified on the subject, and his show reflects that rigour. He interviews witnesses, journalists and scientists about UAP sightings and the disclosure story without tipping into sensationalism. If the Pentagon revelations pulled you in, start here.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode featuring a first-hand military witness — the show's strongest format.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=That+UFO+Podcast">15. That UFO Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/that-ufo-podcast.jpg" alt="That UFO Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Host Andy fronts one of the fastest-moving UFO/UAP shows going, with rapid-reaction coverage of breaking sightings, Congressional hearings and whistleblower testimony. The show to follow if you want the disclosure beat as it actually happens, plus long-form interviews with the field's biggest names.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the most recent hearing reaction episode — that's the show at full tilt.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Strange+Arrivals+Toby+Ball">16. Strange Arrivals</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/strange-arrivals.jpg" alt="Strange Arrivals podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A narrative series about alien abduction that treats the phenomenon as history rather than spectacle — where the stories came from, who told them first, and how the culture around them formed. Tightly seasoned, properly researched, and it never talks down to believers or sceptics.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> season 1, episode 1 — the Betty and Barney Hill case, told from the beginning.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h2>Best cryptid and late-night paranormal radio</h2>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Blurry+Creatures">17. Blurry Creatures</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/blurry-creatures.jpg" alt="Blurry Creatures podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The go-to show for cryptids — Bigfoot, Dogman, Thunderbirds, the Wendigo and every regional monster in between. The hosts blend eyewitness accounts, folklore and a dose of biblical curiosity, and they keep it fun without mocking the people who share their stories.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Dogman episodes — the eyewitness interviews are the best the show has.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Coast+to+Coast+AM+George+Noory">18. Coast to Coast AM</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-paranormal-podcasts/coast-to-coast-am.jpg" alt="Coast to Coast AM podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The granddaddy of American paranormal radio. George Noory takes late-night callers and expert guests across UFOs, ghosts, conspiracies and the unexplained, as Art Bell did before him. Decades of overnight broadcasts have made it a cultural institution — there's no better companion for a long, dark drive.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the classic Art Bell archive episodes if you can find them; otherwise any open-lines night.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h2>Paranormal podcast FAQs</h2>

<h3>What's the best paranormal podcast to start with?</h3>
<p><strong>Uncanny</strong> if you want it investigated properly, <strong>MrBallen</strong> if you want a self-contained story tonight. Both work as a first listen without committing you to a back catalogue.</p>

<h3>What's the difference between a paranormal podcast and a horror podcast?</h3>
<p>Everything on this page is presented as real — witnesses, investigations, first-hand accounts. Horror podcasts are fiction: scripted audio drama and short stories. If that's what you're after, our <a class="text-info" href="/blog/best-horror-podcasts">best horror podcasts</a> list covers it.</p>

<h3>Are there any good UFO podcasts that aren't sensationalist?</h3>
<p>Yes — <strong>Somewhere in the Skies</strong> and <strong>Strange Arrivals</strong> both approach the subject as reporting rather than spectacle, with sourcing you can go and check.</p>
