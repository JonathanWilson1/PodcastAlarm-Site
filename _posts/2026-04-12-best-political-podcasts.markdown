<p>Staying informed without drowning in noise is hard. These political podcasts cut through the headlines with smart analysis, actual expertise and the kind of context you don't get from scrolling. Whether you lean left, right or just want to understand what's going on, there's something here for you.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Pod+Save+America">1. Pod Save America</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/pod-save-america.jpg" alt="Pod Save America podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Former Obama staffers Jon Favreau, Jon Lovett and Tommy Vietor break down American politics with insider knowledge and sharp opinions. They don't pretend to be neutral, which is refreshing. One of the most listened-to political podcasts for good reason.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Rest+Is+Politics">2. The Rest Is Politics</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/the-rest-is-politics.jpg" alt="The Rest Is Politics podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Alastair Campbell and Rory Stewart — one Labour, one Conservative — discuss UK and global politics with genuine respect for each other's positions. The chemistry is brilliant and you'll actually learn something about how government works behind the scenes.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=FiveThirtyEight+Politics+Podcast">3. FiveThirtyEight Politics</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/fivethirtyeight-politics.jpg" alt="FiveThirtyEight Politics podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Data-driven political analysis from the team that made election forecasting mainstream. They dig into polls, demographics and voting patterns with the kind of rigour that makes hot takes feel shallow by comparison. Essential listening during election season.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=NPR+Politics+Podcast">4. The NPR Politics Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/npr-politics.jpg" alt="The NPR Politics Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>NPR's political team delivers quick, clear episodes on the biggest stories in Washington. They publish multiple times a week and are especially strong on breaking news. If you want to stay current without spending hours, this is your best bet.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Slate+Political+Gabfest">5. Political Gabfest</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/political-gabfest.jpg" alt="Political Gabfest podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Slate's long-running roundtable with Emily Bazelon, John Dickerson and David Plotz has been going since 2005. Three smart people argue about politics with depth and good humour. The "cocktail chatter" segment at the end is a bonus — interesting stories you'll want to share.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Pod+Save+the+World">6. Pod Save the World</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/pod-save-the-world.jpg" alt="Pod Save the World podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Tommy Vietor covers foreign policy and national security with genuine expertise from his time in the Obama administration. If you want to understand the geopolitics behind the headlines — from NATO to the Middle East — this is the show. Informed without being dry.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Ezra+Klein+Show">7. The Ezra Klein Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/the-ezra-klein-show.jpg" alt="The Ezra Klein Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ezra Klein's long-form interviews with politicians, thinkers and authors go beyond the news cycle to explore the ideas shaping politics. He's one of the best interviewers in podcasting — genuinely curious and willing to sit with complexity.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Political+Party+Matt+Forde">8. The Political Party</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-political-podcasts/the-political-party.jpg" alt="The Political Party podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Matt Forde interviews politicians from across the spectrum in front of a live audience, mixing comedy with serious political conversation. Former PMs, cabinet ministers and backbenchers all open up in ways they rarely do on TV. A uniquely British take on political podcasting.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
