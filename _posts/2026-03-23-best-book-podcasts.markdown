<p>A good book podcast does what a good bookshop does — puts something in your hands you'd never have found on your own. These 10 shows cover everything from new releases to forgotten classics, and every one of them will wreck your to-read list in the best possible way.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=What+Should+I+Read+Next+Anne+Bogel">1. What Should I Read Next?</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/what-should-i-read-next.jpg" alt="What Should I Read Next podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Anne Bogel asks every guest the same three questions: what do you love, what don't you love, and what are you reading now? Then she recommends three books. The format is simple and addictive. You'll add at least two books to your list every episode.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Stacks+Traci+Thomas">2. The Stacks</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/the-stacks.jpg" alt="The Stacks podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Traci Thomas talks books with authors, critics and fellow readers with infectious enthusiasm and sharp opinions. Her taste is broad — literary fiction, memoirs, nonfiction, genre — and she's not afraid to say when a hyped book doesn't deliver. Honest and deeply well-read.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Backlisted+podcast">3. Backlisted</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/backlisted.jpg" alt="Backlisted podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Andy Miller and John Mitchinson champion books that deserve a second life — forgotten novels, overlooked classics, out-of-print gems. Each episode features a guest author discussing a book they love. It's the literary equivalent of digging through a secondhand bookshop's back room.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Book+Fight+podcast">4. Book Fight!</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/book-fight.jpg" alt="Book Fight podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Two writing professors read a short story every week and argue about whether it's any good. It's funny, opinionated and surprisingly educational about craft. They've been doing it for over a decade now, which means the back catalogue alone will keep you busy for months.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Totally+Booked+with+Zibby">5. Totally Booked with Zibby</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/totally-booked.jpg" alt="Totally Booked with Zibby podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Zibby Owens interviews authors with genuine warmth and curiosity, covering everything from literary fiction to memoirs to debut novels. She's read every book she discusses, asks questions other interviewers miss, and her enthusiasm for reading is completely infectious.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Currently+Reading+podcast+books">6. Currently Reading</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/currently-reading.jpg" alt="Currently Reading podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Kaytee and Meredith share what they're reading right now with the energy of two friends catching up over coffee. Covers new releases, backlist picks and bookish news. If you want a casual, relatable book podcast that feels like a group chat, this is it.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Books+Unbound+podcast">7. Books Unbound</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/books-unbound.jpg" alt="Books Unbound podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A deep dive into one book per episode, pulling apart themes, structure and what makes it stick with you. The hosts bring genuine literary analysis without academic stuffiness. Great for readers who want more than just "should I read this or not."</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Shelf+Discovery+podcast+books">8. Shelf Discovery</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/shelf-discovery.jpg" alt="Shelf Discovery podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A podcast about rediscovering books you loved — or missed — with fresh eyes. Each episode revisits a title and asks whether it holds up. Part nostalgia trip, part literary criticism. Particularly good on YA classics and the books that shaped a generation of readers.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=From+the+Front+Porch+podcast">9. From the Front Porch</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/from-the-front-porch.jpg" alt="From the Front Porch podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Annie Jones runs an independent bookshop in Tennessee and interviews authors on her podcast with the warmth you'd expect from someone who hand-sells books for a living. Conversations are thoughtful, unhurried and always end with a recommendation you'll actually want to follow up on.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Bookshelf+podcast">10. The Bookshelf</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-book-podcasts/the-bookshelf.jpg" alt="The Bookshelf podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Author interviews and book reviews delivered with genuine passion and thoughtful criticism. The Bookshelf covers a wide range of genres and never feels like it's pushing an agenda. A reliable weekly source of reading recommendations that consistently delivers.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
