<p>Whether you're buying your first rental or scaling a portfolio, these podcasts break down real estate investing in a way that actually makes sense. No hype, no guru nonsense — just practical advice from people who've done it.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=BiggerPockets+Real+Estate+Podcast">1. BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/biggerpockets.jpg" alt="BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The biggest real estate podcast out there, and for good reason. David Greene and Rob Abasolo interview investors at every stage — from first-time landlords to people managing hundreds of units. Consistently actionable advice you can use immediately.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Property+Podcast">2. The Property Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/the-property-podcast.jpg" alt="The Property Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Rob Bence and Rob Dix break down UK property investing with a friendly, no-nonsense approach. They cover everything from buy-to-let basics to market analysis, and their weekly format makes it easy to stay current on the UK market.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Afford+Anything+Paula+Pant">3. Afford Anything</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/afford-anything.jpg" alt="Afford Anything podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Paula Pant started with rental properties and expanded into broader financial independence content. Her interviews are thoughtful and she asks the questions most hosts skip. Great for people who want real estate to be part of a bigger financial picture.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Real+Estate+Guys+Radio+Show">4. The Real Estate Guys Radio Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/real-estate-guys.jpg" alt="The Real Estate Guys Radio Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Robert Helms and Russell Gray have been doing this since 1997, which tells you something. They focus on the economics behind real estate — interest rates, market cycles, syndication — and bring on expert guests who genuinely know their stuff.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Rental+Income+Podcast">5. Rental Income Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/rental-income-podcast.jpg" alt="Rental Income Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dan Lane interviews landlords about their rental portfolios — how many units they own, what they earn, and what went wrong along the way. The format is simple but the real numbers and honest stories make it incredibly useful for aspiring landlords.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Millionaire+Mindcast">6. Millionaire Mindcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/millionaire-mindcast.jpg" alt="Millionaire Mindcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Matt Aitchison interviews self-made millionaires, many of whom built their wealth through real estate. The conversations cover deal structures, mindset, and the mistakes that taught them the most. Practical and motivating in equal measure.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Get+Rich+Education">7. Get Rich Education</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/get-rich-education.jpg" alt="Get Rich Education podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Keith Weinhold makes a compelling case for building wealth through real estate rather than traditional retirement accounts. He covers creative financing, cash flow analysis, and macroeconomic trends that affect property investors.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=On+The+Market+BiggerPockets">8. On The Market</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-real-estate-podcasts/on-the-market.jpg" alt="On The Market podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A BiggerPockets spin-off hosted by Dave Meyer that focuses specifically on market data and trends. If you want to understand where the housing market is heading and what the numbers actually mean, this is the show to follow.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
