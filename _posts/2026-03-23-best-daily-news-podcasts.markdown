<p>Scrolling headlines all morning is a terrible way to stay informed. These 10 daily news podcasts give you the full picture in 20 minutes or less — proper journalism, smart analysis and none of the doom-scrolling. Pick one and make it part of your morning.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Daily+New+York+Times">1. The Daily</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/the-daily.jpg" alt="The Daily podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Michael Barbaro and the New York Times team take one major story each day and go deep. The reporting is meticulous, the interviews are revealing, and by the end of each episode you genuinely understand the story — not just the headline.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Up+First+NPR">2. Up First</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/up-first.jpg" alt="Up First from NPR podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>NPR's morning briefing covers the three biggest stories of the day in about 10 minutes. It's crisp, balanced and perfectly timed for a shower or a commute. If you only have time for one news podcast, this is it.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Today+in+Focus+Guardian">3. Today in Focus</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/today-in-focus.jpg" alt="Today in Focus podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Guardian's daily podcast takes one story and gives it 20 minutes of proper attention. Hosted with a UK perspective, it covers everything from Westminster chaos to global crises. The reporting is first-rate and the production keeps getting better.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Post+Reports+Washington+Post">4. Post Reports</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/post-reports.jpg" alt="Post Reports podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Washington Post's daily show features the reporters behind the biggest stories explaining what they found and why it matters. The format puts you right alongside the journalism — you hear the sources, the context and the stakes.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Pod+Save+America">5. Pod Save America</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/pod-save-america.jpg" alt="Pod Save America podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Former Obama staffers Jon Favreau, Jon Lovett, Dan Pfeiffer and Tommy Vietor break down the news with insider knowledge and sharp political analysis. Crooked Media's flagship show is opinionated, well-informed and consistently one of the most-listened-to political podcasts in America.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Intelligence+Economist">6. The Intelligence</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/the-intelligence.jpg" alt="The Intelligence podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Economist's daily podcast covers three global stories in around 20 minutes. The reporting spans continents — you'll hear about elections in South America, tech regulation in Asia and conflict in Africa in the same episode. Genuinely broadens your worldview.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Global+News+Podcast+BBC">7. Global News Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/global-news-bbc.jpg" alt="Global News Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The BBC World Service's flagship podcast drops twice daily with comprehensive international coverage. With correspondents in more countries than any other network, the depth and range is unmatched. A must for anyone who wants news beyond their own borders.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Journal+WSJ+Podcast">8. The Journal</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/the-journal.jpg" alt="The Journal podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Wall Street Journal's daily podcast blends business news with narrative storytelling. Kate Linebaugh and Ryan Knutson make complex financial and corporate stories genuinely gripping. Excellent for understanding how money and power actually move.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+News+Agents">9. The News Agents</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/the-news-agents.jpg" alt="The News Agents podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Emily Maitlis, Jon Sopel and Lewis Goodall deliver sharp, insightful daily news analysis with a UK and global focus. Three veteran journalists who cut through the noise and explain not just what happened, but why it matters. One of the fastest-growing news podcasts in the UK.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Newscast+BBC">10. Newscast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-daily-news-podcasts/newscast-bbc.jpg" alt="Newscast BBC podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The BBC's flagship daily news podcast features their top journalists and correspondents breaking down the biggest stories with insider access. Adam Fleming and the team bring the kind of behind-the-scenes context that makes complex political stories actually make sense.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
