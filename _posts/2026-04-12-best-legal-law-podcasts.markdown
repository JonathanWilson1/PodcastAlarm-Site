<p>The law shapes everything, but most legal coverage is either too dry or too sensational. These podcasts find the sweet spot — making complex legal issues genuinely interesting without dumbing them down. Whether you're a lawyer or just curious, there's something here for you.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Ear+Hustle">1. Ear Hustle</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/ear-hustle.jpg" alt="Ear Hustle podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Created inside San Quentin prison by Earlonne Woods and Nigel Poor, Ear Hustle tells the stories of daily life behind bars. It's a deeply human look at the justice system from the people living in it. Radiotopia production quality with perspectives you won't hear anywhere else.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Strict+Scrutiny+Podcast">2. Strict Scrutiny</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/strict-scrutiny.jpg" alt="Strict Scrutiny podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Three law professors — Leah Litman, Melissa Murray, and Kate Shaw — cover the Supreme Court with sharp analysis and just enough humour. They explain what the Court is actually doing and why it matters, without the dry academic tone you'd expect.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Wrongful+Conviction+Podcast">3. Wrongful Conviction</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/wrongful-conviction.jpg" alt="Wrongful Conviction podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jason Flom interviews people who were wrongfully convicted and the lawyers who fought to free them. Each episode is a gut-punch reminder of how the system can fail, and a celebration of the people who fight to fix it. Important, compelling listening.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Lawfare+Podcast">4. Lawfare Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/lawfare.jpg" alt="Lawfare Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Benjamin Wittes and the Lawfare team cover national security law, cybersecurity, and constitutional issues with the depth they deserve. The guests are top-tier legal scholars and practitioners. Essential for anyone following the intersection of law and government.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Lawyer+2+Lawyer+Legal">5. Lawyer 2 Lawyer</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/lawyer-2-lawyer.jpg" alt="Lawyer 2 Lawyer podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Craig Williams and Bob Ambrogi discuss the week's biggest legal stories with practising lawyers. The format is straightforward — two legal journalists and expert guests breaking down what happened and what it means. Great for keeping up with the legal world.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Amicus+Dahlia+Lithwick">6. Amicus with Dahlia Lithwick</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/amicus.jpg" alt="Amicus with Dahlia Lithwick podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Slate's legal affairs correspondent Dahlia Lithwick covers the Supreme Court and the broader legal landscape with clarity and conviction. Her interviews with judges, lawyers, and scholars make complex constitutional questions feel urgent and relevant.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Make+No+Law+First+Amendment">7. Make No Law</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/make-no-law.jpg" alt="Make No Law podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ken White (Popehat) tells the stories behind landmark First Amendment cases. Each episode takes a Supreme Court decision and reveals the real people and dramatic circumstances that shaped free speech law in America. Brilliantly written and produced.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Slow+Burn+Slate">8. Slow Burn</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-legal-law-podcasts/slow-burn.jpg" alt="Slow Burn podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Each season of Slow Burn unpacks a political and legal scandal in granular detail — Watergate, the Clinton impeachment, the Iraq War. Slate's narrative approach reveals how these crises unfolded day by day, with all the legal drama that came with them.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
