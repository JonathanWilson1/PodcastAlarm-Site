<p>Pregnancy advice on the internet is a minefield. These 10 podcasts cut through the noise with honest, evidence-based guidance from midwives, doctors and parents who've been through it. Whether you're expecting or navigating the first year, these shows have your back.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Mama+Natural+Podcast">1. The Mama Natural Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/mama-natural.jpg" alt="The Mama Natural Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Genevieve Howland covers pregnancy, birth and natural parenting with a research-backed approach. Episodes are warm and reassuring without being preachy, covering everything from nutrition during pregnancy to newborn care with practical advice you can actually use.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Expecting+and+Empowered">2. Expecting and Empowered</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/expecting-empowered.jpg" alt="Expecting and Empowered podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Focused on fitness and wellness during pregnancy and postpartum, this show helps expecting parents stay active safely. Evidence-based guidance on exercise, nutrition and recovery from professionals who specialise in prenatal and postnatal health.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Birth+Hour+Podcast">3. The Birth Hour</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/birth-hour.jpg" alt="The Birth Hour podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Bryn Huntpalmer shares real birth stories from real parents — home births, C-sections, VBACs, the lot. Hearing how other people navigated their experience is surprisingly grounding when you're preparing for your own.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Preggie+Pals+Podcast">4. Preggie Pals</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/preggie-pals.jpg" alt="Preggie Pals podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Part of the New Mommy Media network, Preggie Pals brings together panels of expecting parents and experts to discuss everything from morning sickness to nursery prep. The group format means you get multiple perspectives on every topic.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Evidence+Based+Birth+Podcast">5. Evidence Based Birth</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/evidence-based-birth.jpg" alt="Evidence Based Birth podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dr Rebecca Dekker digs into the actual research behind common pregnancy and birth interventions. If you've ever wondered whether your doctor's recommendation is backed by evidence or just tradition, this podcast will arm you with the facts.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Bump+Podcast">6. The Bump Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/the-bump.jpg" alt="The Bump Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>From the team behind The Bump app, this podcast covers pregnancy and early parenthood with expert guests and a friendly editorial voice. Episodes are short and focused — perfect for squeezing in during a commute or before a nap.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Parenting+Hell+Rob+Beckett">7. Parenting Hell</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/parenting-hell.jpg" alt="Parenting Hell podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Rob Beckett and Josh Widdicombe share the chaotic, hilarious reality of raising kids with celebrity guests who do the same. It's the antidote to perfect-parent culture — honest, very funny and deeply relatable for anyone knee-deep in nappies and tantrums.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=New+Parents+Podcast">8. New Parents Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/new-parents.jpg" alt="New Parents Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Specifically aimed at the overwhelming first year, this show covers sleep, feeding, development milestones and the emotional rollercoaster of becoming a parent. The advice is practical and delivered without condescension.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Good+Inside+Dr+Becky+Kennedy">9. Good Inside with Dr Becky Kennedy</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/good-inside.jpg" alt="Good Inside podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Clinical psychologist Dr Becky Kennedy has become the go-to voice for modern parenting. Her approach — firm boundaries with deep empathy — actually works, and her short episodes give you scripts you can use the same day with your kids.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Informed+Pregnancy+Podcast">10. The Informed Pregnancy Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-pregnancy-podcasts/informed-pregnancy.jpg" alt="The Informed Pregnancy Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dr Elliot Berlin interviews parents about their birth experiences and speaks with experts on pregnancy, birth and postpartum topics. The conversations are honest, empowering and designed to help you make informed decisions about your own journey.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
