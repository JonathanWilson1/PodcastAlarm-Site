Each entry names the episode to start on, so you can try one tonight.</p>

<h2>What's new for 2026</h2>
<p>We've expanded the list from 10 shows to 15 and checked every one is still releasing episodes. The additions cover the gap between celebrity chat and proper long-form interviewing: <strong>Fresh Air</strong>, still the best-prepared interview show in America; <strong>The Louis Theroux Podcast</strong>, which gets uncomfortable in the most productive way; <strong>Where Everybody Knows Your Name</strong> with Ted Danson; <strong>Las Culturistas</strong>, now a genuine A-list stop; and <strong>Happy Sad Confused</strong> for film fans.</p>

<br>

<h2>Best celebrity interview podcasts</h2>
<p>Famous hosts, famous guests, and conversations that go somewhere. These are the shows to start with if you just want a great celebrity interview.</p>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=SmartLess">1. SmartLess</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/smartless.jpg" alt="SmartLess podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jason Bateman, Sean Hayes and Will Arnett take turns surprising each other with a mystery guest. The chemistry between the three hosts is the real draw — the A-list guests are almost a bonus. Consistently one of the funniest interview shows going.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode where the guest is revealed to be a genuine legend — the reaction is half the show.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Armchair+Expert+Dax+Shepard">2. Armchair Expert</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/armchair-expert.jpg" alt="Armchair Expert podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dax Shepard is disarmingly honest about his own flaws, which makes his guests open up in ways they rarely do elsewhere. Conversations go deep on psychology, addiction, relationships and ego. Monica Padman's fact checks at the end are half the fun.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Bradley Cooper episode — two people who've been through the same thing, talking properly about it.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Conan+OBrien+Needs+a+Friend">3. Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/conan-needs-a-friend.jpg" alt="Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Thirty years of late-night experience aimed at an interview format with no time limit and no network notes. Conan is a far better listener than his on-screen persona suggests, and the running bits with producer Matt Gourley and assistant Sona Movsesian are as good as the guests.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Michelle Obama episode — genuinely one of the best interviews in the medium.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Hot+Ones+The+Podcast">4. Hot Ones: The Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/hot-ones-the-podcast.jpg" alt="Hot Ones The Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Sean Evans is one of the best celebrity interviewers alive and his research is legendary. The podcast extends those conversations beyond the hot wings. Guests consistently say it's the best interview they've ever done, and they're usually right.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode with a guest you already like — the deep-cut questions land harder that way.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Call+Her+Daddy+Alex+Cooper">5. Call Her Daddy</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/call-her-daddy.jpg" alt="Call Her Daddy podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Alex Cooper turned a college dorm show into one of the biggest interview podcasts in the world. She books huge guests — athletes, musicians, actors, even presidential candidates — and pushes conversations into places other interviewers avoid. Blunt, entertaining and massively popular for a reason.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any of the big political or pop-star bookings; they're the episodes that made the news.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Good+Hang+with+Amy+Poehler">6. Good Hang with Amy Poehler</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/good-hang-amy-poehler.jpg" alt="Good Hang with Amy Poehler podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Amy Poehler's 2025 debut is exactly what the title promises — a genuinely good hang. She brings famous friends like Tina Fey, Ike Barinholtz and Natasha Lyonne on for loose, warm, very funny conversations. The comic timing you'd expect, plus real curiosity about how people work.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Tina Fey episode — thirty years of friendship doing the heavy lifting.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Where+Everybody+Knows+Your+Name+Ted+Danson">7. Where Everybody Knows Your Name</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/where-everybody-knows-your-name.jpg" alt="Where Everybody Knows Your Name with Ted Danson and Woody Harrelson podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ted Danson interviews people he's curious about, with Woody Harrelson turning up when he feels like it — which is the joke in the subtitle and also genuinely how it works. Danson is unhurried and completely without ego, so guests relax fast. One of the most pleasant listens on this list.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode with a Cheers-era guest; the reminiscing is worth the ticket.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Las+Culturistas+Matt+Rogers+Bowen+Yang">8. Las Culturistas</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/las-culturistas.jpg" alt="Las Culturistas podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Matt Rogers and Bowen Yang talk culture with a guest each week, and the show has quietly become a real A-list stop. The recurring "I don't think so, honey" segment is the best closing bit in podcasting, and the hosts' friendship makes even a thin guest booking work.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode ending in the Culture Awards — that's the show at maximum velocity.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Table+Manners+Jessie+Ware">9. Table Manners</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/table-manners.jpg" alt="Table Manners podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Jessie Ware and her mum Lennie cook dinner for a celebrity guest, and the result is always warmer and more revealing than a studio interview. The food chat alone is worth it. A uniquely British take on the celebrity podcast.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode where Lennie decides she doesn't approve of the guest's eating habits.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h2>Best long-form interview podcasts</h2>
<p>Where the celebrity booking is the start of the conversation rather than the whole of it. If you searched for the best interview podcasts generally, this is the section you want.</p>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=WTF+with+Marc+Maron">10. WTF with Marc Maron</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/wtf-marc-maron.jpg" alt="WTF with Marc Maron podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Marc Maron basically invented the celebrity podcast interview from his garage. Over a thousand episodes in, he still gets raw, unfiltered conversations out of actors, comedians and musicians. The Obama episode is famous, but the lesser-known ones are often better.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Robin Williams episode — the interview everyone points to, and it holds up.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Fresh+Air+NPR+Terry+Gross">11. Fresh Air</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/fresh-air-npr.jpg" alt="Fresh Air podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Terry Gross has been doing this longer and better than almost anyone. The preparation is obsessive, the questions are specific, and she will politely ask the thing a publicist told her not to. If you want a masterclass in the form rather than a chat between famous friends, start here.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any actor interview tied to a film you've seen — the specificity is the point.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=The+Louis+Theroux+Podcast">12. The Louis Theroux Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/louis-theroux-podcast.jpg" alt="The Louis Theroux Podcast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Theroux brings the same trick from his documentaries — the polite, slightly awkward question that nobody else would ask — to long-form celebrity interviews. Guests routinely say more than they meant to. The most consistently newsworthy interview podcast running.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> season 1 — the early episodes set the tone and several made headlines on release.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=The+Diary+of+a+CEO+Steven+Bartlett">13. The Diary of a CEO</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/diary-of-a-ceo.jpg" alt="The Diary of a CEO podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Steven Bartlett interviews everyone from neuroscientists to pop stars, and the conversations are long, detailed and rarely surface-level. He asks the questions most hosts avoid. It's become one of the UK's biggest podcasts for good reason.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any episode where the guest is a name you know from music or sport rather than business.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Club+Shay+Shay+Shannon+Sharpe">14. Club Shay Shay</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/club-shay-shay.jpg" alt="Club Shay Shay podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Shannon Sharpe brings the energy from his sports broadcasting career into long-form interviews with athletes, entertainers and cultural figures. He isn't afraid to ask uncomfortable questions, and his guests clearly respect him for it.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> the Katt Williams episode — one of the most-watched podcast interviews ever made.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h2>Best celebrity podcast for film and TV fans</h2>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/us/search?term=Happy+Sad+Confused+Josh+Horowitz">15. Happy Sad Confused</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-celebrity-podcasts/happy-sad-confused.jpg" alt="Happy Sad Confused podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Josh Horowitz has interviewed more or less everyone in Hollywood, and the podcast is where those conversations get room to breathe. He's a genuine fan without being a pushover, which is a harder balance than it looks. The best place to hear an actor talk properly about the work.</p>
<p><strong>Start with:</strong> any press-tour interview for a film you loved — he'll ask the thing you were wondering about.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h2>Celebrity podcast FAQs</h2>

<h3>What is the best celebrity interview podcast?</h3>
<p><strong>SmartLess</strong> for entertainment, <strong>Armchair Expert</strong> for depth, and <strong>Fresh Air</strong> if you want to hear the form done to its highest standard. All three are free and none require you to start at episode one.</p>

<h3>Which celebrity podcast has the biggest guests?</h3>
<p><strong>SmartLess</strong> and <strong>Call Her Daddy</strong> consistently book the biggest names. <strong>Hot Ones: The Podcast</strong> punches above its weight because guests actively want to do it.</p>

<h3>Are these celebrity podcasts free?</h3>
<p>Yes — all 15 are free on Apple Podcasts and other major apps. Some run paid tiers with ad-free or bonus episodes, but the main feed costs nothing.</p>
