<p>Whether you want sharp tactical analysis, dressing room stories or just a good laugh about the beautiful game, there's a football podcast for you. These 10 shows cover the Premier League, European football and everything in between — and they're all worth a regular listen.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Totally+Football+Show">1. The Totally Football Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/totally-football-show.jpg" alt="The Totally Football Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>James Richardson hosts a rotating panel of top football journalists covering the day's biggest stories. Smart, funny and perfectly paced — it drops daily during the season, so you're never behind on the conversation.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=That+Peter+Crouch+Podcast">2. That Peter Crouch Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/peter-crouch.jpg" alt="That Peter Crouch Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Peter Crouch, Tom Fordyce and Chris Stark deliver behind-the-scenes football stories with genuine humour. Crouch is surprisingly brilliant at this — honest, self-deprecating and full of stories you won't hear anywhere else.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Guardian+Football+Weekly">3. The Guardian Football Weekly</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/guardian-football-weekly.jpg" alt="The Guardian Football Weekly podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Max Rushden leads The Guardian's long-running football podcast with a panel of their best football writers. The analysis is sharp, the banter is natural, and they cover European leagues properly — not just the Premier League.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Men+in+Blazers+podcast">4. Men in Blazers</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/men-in-blazers.jpg" alt="Men in Blazers podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Roger Bennett and Michael Davies cover football from a uniquely Anglo-American perspective. Witty, passionate and unashamedly emotional about the game. They've done more to grow football in America than almost anyone else in media.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Stick+to+Football+Gary+Neville">5. Stick to Football</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/stick-to-football.jpg" alt="Stick to Football podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Gary Neville, Ian Wright, Jamie Carragher and Jill Scott talk football and beyond with the chemistry of a group who genuinely enjoy each other's company. The conversations are relaxed and revealing — guests open up in ways they rarely do on TV.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Football+Ramble+podcast">6. Football Ramble</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/football-ramble.jpg" alt="Football Ramble podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>One of the original football podcasts and still one of the best. The Ramble crew mix match analysis with tangents, running jokes and genuine camaraderie. It feels like listening in on a conversation between mates who really know their football.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Tifo+Football+Podcast">7. Tifo Football Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/tifo-football.jpg" alt="Tifo Football Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Athletic's Tifo team deliver concise, well-researched episodes on tactics, transfers and the business side of football. Episodes are tight — usually under 30 minutes — and consistently informative without being dry.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Athletic+Football+Podcast">8. The Athletic Football Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/athletic-football.jpg" alt="The Athletic Football Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The Athletic's flagship football show features their team of journalists breaking down the biggest stories, transfers and matches. Deep reporting you won't get from the tabloids, delivered in a conversational style.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Football+Podcast+BBC+5+Live">9. The Football Podcast (BBC 5 Live)</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/bbc-5-live-football.jpg" alt="BBC 5 Live Football Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The BBC's Premier League podcast with Mark Chapman and a rotating panel of pundits and journalists. Reliable, well-produced and drops after every major round of fixtures. A solid staple for anyone following the English game.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Quickly+Kevin+Will+He+Score">10. Quickly Kevin, Will He Score?</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-football-podcasts/quickly-kevin.jpg" alt="Quickly Kevin Will He Score podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Josh Widdicombe, Chris Scull and Michael Mayall dive deep into 90s and early 2000s football nostalgia. Obscure transfers, forgotten kit sponsors and players you'd completely erased from memory. Pure joy for anyone who grew up watching Football Italia.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
