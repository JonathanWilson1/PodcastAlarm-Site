<p>If your brain won't switch off at bedtime, a sleep podcast can work wonders. These shows use calming voices, gentle stories and ambient sounds to help you drift off naturally — no meditation experience required.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<h3>What's new this August 2026</h3>
<p>Sleep With Me and Nothing Much Happens remain the gold standard for bedtime listening — both have grown their libraries significantly, so there's always a fresh episode to drift off to no matter how many you've heard. This update also adds cover art for every show so you can spot your next listen at a glance.</p>

<p>The one catch with a sleep podcast: it keeps playing all night and drains your battery. <a class="text-info" href="https://apps.apple.com/app/podcast-alarm/id1441890371?ct={{ page.permalink | slugify }}&amp;mt=8"><strong>Podcast Alarm</strong></a> fixes that — set a sleep timer so your show fades out after you've drifted off, then wake up to a different podcast in the morning.</p>
<br>

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Sleep+With+Me">1. Sleep With Me</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-to-fall-asleep-to/sleep-with-me.jpg" alt="Sleep With Me podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The original sleep podcast. Host Drew Ackerman tells long, meandering, deliberately boring bedtime stories in a soothing monotone. It sounds strange, but it works — the stories are just interesting enough to distract your racing mind, but too dull to keep you awake. Over a billion downloads and counting.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Nothing+Much+Happens">2. Nothing Much Happens</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-to-fall-asleep-to/nothing-much-happens.jpg" alt="Nothing Much Happens podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Kathryn Nicolai reads original bedtime stories for adults. Each episode paints a cosy scene — walking through autumn leaves, sitting by a fire, wandering through a quiet village — and then reads it twice, more slowly the second time. The effect is genuinely hypnotic.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Get+Sleepy">3. Get Sleepy</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-to-fall-asleep-to/get-sleepy.jpg" alt="Get Sleepy podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A mix of guided meditation and sleep stories, Get Sleepy starts each episode with a short wind-down before easing into a calming narrative. The production quality is excellent, with subtle ambient sounds layered beneath the narration. New episodes three times a week.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Slow+Radio+BBC">4. Slow Radio — BBC</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-to-fall-asleep-to/slow-radio.jpg" alt="Slow Radio BBC podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>No narration, no music — just real-world sounds. Dawn birdsong in a Scottish forest. Rain on a tin roof. Waves on a Cornish beach. The BBC captures natural soundscapes from around the world, and the result is deeply relaxing. Perfect if voices keep you awake.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Moth">5. The Moth</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-to-fall-asleep-to/the-moth.jpg" alt="The Moth podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Real people telling true stories on stage, The Moth isn't designed as a sleep podcast — but its warm, conversational storytelling makes it perfect for winding down. The stories are engaging enough to stop you doomscrolling, but gentle enough to let you drift off.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Boring+Books+for+Bedtime">6. Boring Books for Bedtime</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-to-fall-asleep-to/boring-books-for-bedtime.jpg" alt="Boring Books for Bedtime podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Exactly what it sounds like. Public domain texts read in a calm, steady voice — think Victorian gardening manuals, old geography textbooks, and forgotten etiquette guides. The content is wonderfully dull, and that's the whole point.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Deep+Energy+Jim+Butler">7. Deep Energy</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-podcasts-to-fall-asleep-to/deep-energy.jpg" alt="Deep Energy podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ambient electronic music designed for sleep, meditation, and deep relaxation. No words, no stories — just long, flowing soundscapes that last 1–2 hours. If you want something playing in the background all night, this is ideal.</p>
