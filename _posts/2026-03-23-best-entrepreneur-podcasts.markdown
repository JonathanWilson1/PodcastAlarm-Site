<p>Building something from scratch is lonely enough without having to figure everything out yourself. These 10 podcasts put you in the room with founders, bootstrappers and investors who share what actually worked — and what nearly killed their companies. Here are the best shows for entrepreneurs right now.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=How+I+Built+This+Guy+Raz">1. How I Built This</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/how-i-built-this.jpg" alt="How I Built This podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Guy Raz interviews the founders behind the world's biggest brands — Airbnb, Spanx, Dyson — and traces the full journey from idea to empire. Every episode has a moment where you think "they nearly didn't make it." Endlessly inspiring.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=My+First+Million+Podcast">2. My First Million</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/my-first-million.jpg" alt="My First Million podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Sam Parr and Shaan Puri brainstorm business ideas live on air with infectious energy. Half the fun is hearing which ideas they'd actually put money behind. If you're the type who sees business opportunities everywhere, this show will supercharge that instinct.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Masters+of+Scale+Reid+Hoffman">3. Masters of Scale</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/masters-of-scale.jpg" alt="Masters of Scale podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman explores how companies grow from zero to massive scale. The production quality is exceptional — think narrative storytelling meets MBA-level strategy. Each episode builds around a single counterintuitive lesson about scaling.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+GaryVee+Audio+Experience">4. The GaryVee Audio Experience</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/garyvee.jpg" alt="The GaryVee Audio Experience podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Gary Vaynerchuk delivers raw, unfiltered advice on marketing, social media and hustle culture. Whether you love his intensity or find it exhausting, his practical takes on attention and content strategy are hard to argue with.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Indie+Hackers+Podcast">5. Indie Hackers Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/indie-hackers.jpg" alt="Indie Hackers Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Courtland Allen interviews bootstrapped founders who built profitable businesses without venture capital. The conversations are refreshingly honest about revenue numbers, mistakes and the realities of going it alone. Essential listening for anyone building a solo or small-team product.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Starter+Story+Podcast">6. Starter Story</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/starter-story.jpg" alt="Starter Story podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Pat Walls interviews founders of small but profitable businesses — often ones you've never heard of. The focus on real revenue numbers and step-by-step breakdowns makes this far more actionable than most founder interview shows.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+100+MBA+Show+Podcast">7. The $100 MBA Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/100-mba.jpg" alt="The $100 MBA Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Omar Zenhom delivers short, focused business lessons in under 15 minutes. Each episode tackles one specific topic — pricing, email marketing, hiring — with clear takeaways. Perfect for busy founders who want to learn without committing to a two-hour episode.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Entrepreneurs+on+Fire+John+Lee+Dumas">8. Entrepreneurs on Fire</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/entrepreneurs-on-fire.jpg" alt="Entrepreneurs on Fire podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>John Lee Dumas has published over 3,000 episodes interviewing entrepreneurs across every industry imaginable. The daily format means there's always a fresh conversation waiting. His signature questions — worst moment, aha moment, best advice — consistently draw out gold.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Side+Hustle+School+Chris+Guillebeau">9. Side Hustle School</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/side-hustle-school.jpg" alt="Side Hustle School podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Chris Guillebeau shares one new side hustle story every day in under 10 minutes. The ideas range from brilliantly simple to genuinely surprising, and each episode breaks down how much it cost to start and how much it earns. No excuses — just proof it can be done.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=StartUp+Podcast+Gimlet">10. StartUp Podcast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-entrepreneur-podcasts/startup-gimlet.jpg" alt="StartUp Podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Alex Blumberg documented the messy, nerve-wracking process of building Gimlet Media in real time. The first season is one of the best pieces of audio storytelling about entrepreneurship ever made — raw, vulnerable and utterly compelling.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
