<p>Good investing advice doesn't have to come from a financial advisor. These podcasts cover everything from index funds to individual stock picks, with hosts who explain their thinking clearly enough that you can actually learn from it.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=We+Study+Billionaires">1. We Study Billionaires</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/we-study-billionaires.jpg" alt="We Study Billionaires podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>The flagship show from The Investors Podcast Network, hosted by Stig Brodersen and Clay Finck. They study the strategies of Warren Buffett, Ray Dalio, and other legendary investors, then break down how to apply those lessons. Deep, research-driven episodes.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Motley+Fool+Money">2. Motley Fool Money</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/motley-fool-money.jpg" alt="Motley Fool Money podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>A weekly roundup of the biggest stories in the stock market. The Motley Fool analysts discuss earnings, market moves, and individual stocks with a long-term investing mindset. Quick episodes that keep you informed without overwhelming you.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Rational+Reminder+Podcast">3. Rational Reminder</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/rational-reminder.jpg" alt="Rational Reminder podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ben Felix and Cameron Passmore take an evidence-based approach to investing, grounding every recommendation in academic research. If you want to understand why index funds beat stock picking, or how factor investing works, this is the podcast.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Animal+Spirits+Podcast">4. Animal Spirits</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/animal-spirits.jpg" alt="Animal Spirits podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Michael Batnick and Ben Carlson discuss markets, investing, and the economy in a conversational style that feels like two smart friends talking over coffee. They cover both the data and the behavioural side of investing, which makes for a well-rounded show.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Money+Guy+Show">5. Money Guy Show</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/money-guy.jpg" alt="Money Guy Show podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Brian Preston and Bo Hanson are financial advisors who share wealth-building strategies based on decades of experience with real clients. Their "Financial Order of Operations" framework is one of the clearest guides to prioritising where your money should go.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=InvestED+Podcast+Phil+Town">6. InvestED</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/invested.jpg" alt="InvestED podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Phil Town and his daughter Danielle learn value investing together, making the show perfect for beginners. Phil is a hedge fund manager who explains concepts like moats, margin of safety, and intrinsic value in simple terms. Warm and educational.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Masters+in+Business+Bloomberg">7. Masters in Business</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/masters-in-business.jpg" alt="Masters in Business podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Barry Ritholtz interviews the biggest names in finance — fund managers, economists, authors, and CEOs. The conversations are long and detailed, and Barry's experience means he asks the right questions. A Bloomberg production with serious guests every week.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Equity+Mates+Investing">8. Equity Mates Investing</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-investing-podcasts/equity-mates.jpg" alt="Equity Mates Investing podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Two mates from Australia who started investing and decided to learn in public. They interview fund managers, analysts, and authors, and they're honest about what they don't know. Great for newer investors who want to learn alongside the hosts.</p>
