<p>Whether you're swiping, situationshipping or years deep into a relationship that needs a tune-up, these podcasts have something for you. Therapists, comedians and people who've been through it all — here are the 10 best dating and relationship podcasts right now.</p>

{% include callToActionRow.html %}

<br>

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Where+Should+We+Begin+Esther+Perel">1. Where Should We Begin</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/where-should-we-begin.jpg" alt="Where Should We Begin podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Esther Perel records real therapy sessions with anonymous couples and the result is unlike anything else in podcasting. You hear people say things to each other they've never said before. Riveting, uncomfortable and genuinely moving.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=U+Up+Betches">2. U Up?</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/u-up.jpg" alt="U Up? podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Betches Media's dating podcast tackles app culture, texting etiquette and all the modern nonsense with sharp humour. They answer listener questions with brutal honesty and it's exactly the friend advice you wish you had.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Savage+Lovecast+Dan+Savage">3. Savage Lovecast</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/savage-lovecast.jpg" alt="Savage Lovecast podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Dan Savage has been giving no-nonsense relationship and sex advice for decades and the podcast version is just as sharp. Callers phone in with their messiest situations and Dan tells them what they need to hear, not what they want to hear.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Modern+Love+New+York+Times">4. Modern Love</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/modern-love.jpg" alt="Modern Love podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Based on the beloved New York Times column, each episode brings a real essay about love to life — read by the author, then discussed. The stories range from heartbreaking to hilarious, and they always feel deeply human.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Girls+Gotta+Eat">5. Girls Gotta Eat</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/girls-gotta-eat.jpg" alt="Girls Gotta Eat podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Ashley Hesseltine and Rayna Greenberg tackle dating, sex and relationships with zero filter and maximum laughs. They bring on therapists, sexperts and comedians, and their listener stories segments are legendary. Honest, hilarious and unapologetically direct.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Heart+podcast">6. The Heart</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/the-heart.jpg" alt="The Heart podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>An audio art project about intimacy in all its forms — desire, identity, the body. It's experimental, beautifully produced and nothing like a typical relationship podcast. If you want something that makes you feel rather than advise, this is it.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Dear+Sugars+podcast">7. Dear Sugars</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/dear-sugars.jpg" alt="Dear Sugars podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Cheryl Strayed and Steve Almond answer listener letters about love, heartbreak and the messy business of being human. The advice is wise, compassionate and often beautifully expressed. Think of it as an advice column read aloud by two people who genuinely care.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Dates+and+Mates+Damona+Hoffman">8. Dates & Mates</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/dates-and-mates.jpg" alt="Dates & Mates podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Certified dating coach Damona Hoffman breaks down headlines, answers listener questions and gives genuinely practical dating advice. She's been doing this for years and it shows — no fluff, just useful strategies that actually work.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=Love+Letters+Boston+Globe">9. Love Letters</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/love-letters.jpg" alt="Love Letters podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Based on the Boston Globe's long-running advice column, Meredith Goldstein takes real reader dilemmas about love and relationships and discusses them with warmth and wit. It feels like eavesdropping on the best conversations your friends never had.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">

<h3><a class="text-info" href="https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/search?term=The+Viall+Files+Nick+Viall">10. The Viall Files</a></h3>
<img src="/images/blog/charts/best-dating-podcasts/the-viall-files.jpg" alt="The Viall Files podcast artwork" width="100" style="border-radius:12px;float:left;margin:0 15px 15px 0;">
<p>Former Bachelor Nick Viall parlayed reality TV into a surprisingly thoughtful advice show. Listeners call in with dating problems and Nick gives direct, no-sugar-coating feedback. He's better at this than he was at finding love on camera.</p>
<br style="clear:both;">
